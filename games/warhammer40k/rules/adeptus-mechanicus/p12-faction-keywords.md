# FACTION KEYWORDS:

#### **HASTARII EXTERMINATORS**

_These heavily armoured Skitarii lay down withering firepower with heightened efficiency. The expanding energy cones of their eradication casters disrupt the target at a molecular level, grossly maiming or completely obliterating their victims. Combined with their shoulder-mounted arc blasters, this arsenal allows Hastarii Exterminators to blast the heart from the enemy battle line._
