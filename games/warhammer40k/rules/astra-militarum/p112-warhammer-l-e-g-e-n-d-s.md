# WARHAMMER L E G E N D S

#### **CRASSUS**

###### **WARHAMMER** L E G E N D S

_The armoured personnel carrier known as the Crassus takes its name from the legendary general Borgen Crassus, who remained steadfastly loyal to the Imperium throughout the grim and bloody Macharian Heresy. Heavily armoured and deceptively fast, the Crassus remains an exemplar of troop transport design despite its venerable age._

###### **WARGEAR OPTIONS**

- This model can be equipped with one of the following:

- 1 heavy stubber

- 1 storm bolter

KEYWORDS: **Vehicle, Frame, Imperium, Squadron, Transport, Smoke, Crassus**
