###### **`DETACHMENT RULES`**

###### **DESIGNATED TARGETS**

_A regiment's resilient and often unconventional scouts are deployed to provide forward intelligence on enemy dispositions. With a combination of regimental issue scopes and guerrilla instincts hard-won through survival at the forefronts of war zones, they can draw massed firepower down on lurking threats._

###### Friendly **`SCOUT SENTINEL/ASTRA MILITARUM INFANTRY SMOKE`** units have the following ability:

**Signal Flares:** In your Shooting phase, this unit can select one **visible** enemy unit within 12". That enemy unit is **designated** : - While a unit is **designated** , that unit has +3" **detection range** .

This **detachment** has the **`RECON`** tag and cannot be taken with another **`RECON` detachment** .
