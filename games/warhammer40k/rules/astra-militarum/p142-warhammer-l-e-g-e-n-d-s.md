# WARHAMMER L E G E N D S

#### **STORMBLADE**

###### **WARHAMMER** L E G E N D S

_Designed to fulfil a Titan-hunting role similar to that of the Shadowsword, the Stormblade's main armament is the plasma blastgun. Though less stable than a volcano cannon, this huge weapon can be turned upon titanic war machines, infantry formations or armoured bastions with equally devastating results._

###### **WARGEAR OPTIONS**

- This model's 2 twin heavy bolters can be replaced with 2 twin heavy flamers

- This model can be equipped with 1 hunter-killer missile

- This model can be equipped with one of the following:

- 1 heavy stubber

- 1 storm bolter

- This model can be equipped with one of the following:

- 2 lascannons and 2 twin heavy bolters

- 2 lascannons and 2 twin heavy flamers

KEYWORDS: **Vehicle, Titanic, Frame, Imperium, Smoke, Stormblade**
