# WARHAMMER L E G E N D S

#### **COLOSSUS**

###### **WARHAMMER** L E G E N D S

_The Colossus is a relic weapon of ancient provenance whose massive damage output more than compensates for its ponderous rate of fire. Colossus are amongst the largest artillery pieces fielded by the Astra Militarum, and a direct hit from a Colossus shell can turn the staunchest bunker into a smoking caldera._

###### **WARGEAR OPTIONS**

- This model can be equipped with 1 hunter-killer missile

- This model can be equipped with one of the following:

- 1 heavy stubber

- 1 storm bolter
