# WARHAMMER L E G E N D S

#### **EARTHSHAKER CARRIAGE BATTERY**

###### **WARHAMMER** L E G E N D S

_Deployed far behind the front line, Earthshaker Carriages can maintain a relentless rate of fire, limited only by the ready supply of munitions Such barrages have been known to break enemy positions almost unaided; heavy shelling reduces strongholds to rubble and churns contested ground into cratered wastes._

###### **WARGEAR OPTIONS**

###### - None

KEYWORDS: **Infantry, Artillery, Frame, Imperium, Grenades, Regiment, Earthshaker Carriage Battery**
