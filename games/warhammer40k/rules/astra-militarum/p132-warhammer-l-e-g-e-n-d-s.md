# WARHAMMER L E G E N D S

#### **MARAUDER BOMBER**

###### **WARHAMMER** L E G E N D S

_Capable of atmospheric and void operations, the Marauder Bomber is an Imperial Navy workhorse with an excellent weapon payload. It excels when supporting Astra Militarum ground offensives with bombing runs on enemy troop concentrations, entrenched positions and supply facilities._
