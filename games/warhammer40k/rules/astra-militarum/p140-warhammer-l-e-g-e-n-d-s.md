# WARHAMMER L E G E N D S

#### **RAPIER LASER DESTROYER BATTERY**

###### **WARHAMMER** L E G E N D S

_The Rapier carrier is a bulky tracked device that traces its origin to the dawn of Mankind's stellar empire. Compatible with various heavy weapons, the Rapier is most commonly fitted with a powerful quad lascannon known as a laser destroyer, making it a compact but potent anti-armour asset._
