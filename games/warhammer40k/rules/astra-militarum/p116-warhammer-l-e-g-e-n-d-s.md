#### **HADES BREACHING DRILL**

###### **WARHAMMER** L E G E N D S

_Originally designed for industrial mining, the Hades Breaching Drill has proved itself to be a valuable weapon of siegecraft. The machine's powerful melta-cutter is capable of pounding through earth, rock and even reinforced ferrocrete, boring a tunnel broad enough for troops to assault through and mangling any living foes that block its path._
