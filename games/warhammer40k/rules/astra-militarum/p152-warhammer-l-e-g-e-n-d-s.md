# WARHAMMER L E G E N D S

#### **VULTURE GUNSHIP**

###### **WARHAMMER** L E G E N D S

_Based on the Valkyrie STC, Vulture Gunships are high-speed, low-level hunter-killers capable of bearing a wide range of payloads. Whether performing close-protection roles for drop troops or strafing missions against massed infantry and light vehicles, Vultures are stalwarts of the Imperial Navy that can be adapted to many combat situations._
