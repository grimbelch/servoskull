# WARHAMMER L E G E N D S

#### **MEDUSA CARRIAGE BATTERY**

###### **WARHAMMER** L E G E N D S

_Few walls are thick enough to withstand the artillery fire launched from a Medusa siege cannon. Able to fire heavy shells that can reduce structures to rubble, many war zones resonate with the sonorous fire of the Medusa's siege cannons, levelling battlefields until there is nowhere for the hapless enemy to hide._
