# HIPPOGRIFF AFV

#### **HIPPOGRIFF AFV**

_The Hippogriff Armoured Fighting Vehicle combines speed and agility with a range of turret-mounted weapon loadouts capable of handling almost any battlefield situation. Excellent rapid-response vehicles well suited to escort duties, reconnaissance in force, and armoured countercharges, they have been the bane of foes many times their size._

###### **UNIT COMPOSITION**
