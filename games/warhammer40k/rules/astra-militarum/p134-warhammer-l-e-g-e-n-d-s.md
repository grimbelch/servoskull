# WARHAMMER L E G E N D S

#### **MARAUDER DESTROYER**

###### **WARHAMMER** L E G E N D S

_The Marauder Destroyer was developed during the Second War for Armageddon in response to the dominance of Ork air power. The result was a heavily armed and armoured aircraft capable of hunting down airborne foes or striking armoured ground targets with an array of fearsome guns and missiles._

###### **WARGEAR OPTIONS**
