#### **DEATH COMPANY MARINES** _WITH BOLTGUNS_

###### **WARHAMMER** L E G E N D S

_Members of the Death Company are possessed of a berserk fury, driven insane by terrible visions and hallucinations. They seek nothing but death in battle, and such is their ferocity that they barely flinch at even the most grievous of injuries, thinking of nothing but the destruction of their enemies._

- Any number of models can each have their bolt pistol replaced with one of the following:

- 1 hand flamer

If a **Chaplain** model from your army with the Leader ability can be attached to a **Tactical Squad** , it can be attached to this unit instead

- 1 plasma pistol
