###### **`DETACHMENT RULES`**

###### **WRATH OF ANGELS**

_Few living warriors embody the ideal of the Emperor's wrathful angels more than the Sanguinary Guard, for they appear without warning to wreak death and destruction before surging on to the next foe with shocking speed._

When a friendly **`SANGUINARY GUARD`** unit is selected to make a **fall-back move** , that move does not prevent that unit from being **eligible to shoot/eligible to declare a charge** .
