### **ARRIVING FROM STRATEGIC RESERVES** 20.03

To arrive on the battlefield, each **strategic reserves** unit must make an **ingress move** (see below). Unless otherwise stated, they can only do so from the second battle round onwards.

###### **`INGRESS MOVE`** 20.04

<!-- Start of picture text -->
SET-UP DISTANCE: 6"<br>ELIGIBLE IF: Your unit is in strategic reserves (excluding units<br>that are embarked within TRANSPORTS that are themselves in<br>strategic reserves ).<br>EFFECT: Your unit is set up as described in Set Up (03.02).<br>WHILE MOVING: Set up your unit wholly within the set-up<br>distance of one or more battlefield edges and more than 8"<br>horizontally from all enemy units.<br>- Before the Third Battle Round: While doing so,<br>no models can be set up within your opponent's<br>deployment zone.<br>AFTER MOVING: Unless otherwise stated, until the start of the<br>next Charge phase, your unit is not eligible to make any other<br>type of move.<br><!-- End of picture text -->

At the end of the third battle round, unless otherwise stated, all **strategic reserves** units that have not made one or more **ingress moves** are **destroyed** , with the following exceptions:

- Units embarked within **`TRANSPORTS`** that have made an **ingress move** during the battle.

- **Repositioned** units (see opposite).
