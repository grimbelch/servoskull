###### **`ASSAULT SHOOTING`** 10.05

<!-- Start of picture text -->
ELIGIBLE IF: All of the following apply to your unit:<br>- Unengaged and made an advance move this turn.<br>- Has one or more [ASSAULT] weapons.<br>EFFECT: Your unit shoots as described in Making Attacks (04).<br>WHILE SHOOTING: You can only select [ASSAULT] weapons to<br>make attacks with.<br>AFTER SHOOTING: Until the end of the phase, your unit is<br>not eligible to start an action .<br><!-- End of picture text -->

###### **`CLOSE-QUARTERS SHOOTING`** 10.06

<!-- Start of picture text -->
ELIGIBLE IF: All of the following apply to your unit:<br>- Engaged and did not make an advance move this turn.<br>- Has one or more [CLOSE-QUARTERS] weapons or is a<br>MONSTER/VEHICLE unit.<br>EFFECT: Your unit shoots as described in Making Attacks (04).<br>WHILE SHOOTING: Models in your unit can target enemy units<br>your unit is engaged with.<br>- MONSTER / VEHICLE Models: Each time a MONSTER / VEHICLE<br>model in your unit makes an attack:<br>▫ Unless that attack is made with a [CLOSE-QUARTERS]<br>weapon and targets a unit your unit is engaged with,<br>subtract 1 from the hit roll .<br>▫ If that attack is made with a [BLAST] weapon, it still<br>cannot target a unit your unit is engaged with.<br>- Non- MONSTER /Non- VEHICLE Models: You can only select<br>[CLOSE-QUARTERS] weapons to make attacks with and you<br>can only select enemy units that are engaged with your<br>unit as targets.<br>AFTER SHOOTING: Until the end of the phase, your unit is<br>not eligible to start an action .<br><!-- End of picture text -->

###### **`INDIRECT SHOOTING`** 10.07

<!-- Start of picture text -->
ELIGIBLE IF: All of the following apply to your unit:<br>- Unengaged and did not make an advance move this turn.<br>- Has one or more [INDIRECT FIRE] weapons.<br>EFFECT: Your unit shoots as described in Making Attacks (04).<br>WHILE SHOOTING:<br>- [INDIRECT FIRE] weapons in your unit can target units<br>that are not visible to the attacking model.<br>- Each time an [INDIRECT FIRE] weapon makes an attack:<br>▫ The target has the benefit of cover against that<br>attack (13.08).<br>▫ You cannot re-roll hit rolls .<br>▫ An unmodified hit roll of 1-5 fails, unless your unit<br>remained stationary this turn and the target is<br>visible to one or more friendly units, in which case an<br>unmodified hit roll of 1-3 fails instead.<br>AFTER SHOOTING: Until the end of the phase, your unit is<br>not eligible to start an action .<br><!-- End of picture text -->

###### **INDIRECT FIRE**

_When you select_ **_indirect shooting_** _for a unit, its_ **`[INDIRECT FIRE]`** _weapons can launch punishing barrages on targets that are not_ **_visible_** _, but don't forget that its other weapons can still target other_ **_visible_** _targets._
