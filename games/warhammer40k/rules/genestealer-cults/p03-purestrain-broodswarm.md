# UNIQUE: PURESTRAIN <u>PURESTRAIN BROODSWARM</u>

```
PURESTRAIN BROODS ARE UNLEASHED EN MASSE TO TEAR THE HEART FROM THE ENEMY ARMY
```

###### **`DETACHMENT RULES`**

##### **ENEMY WITHIN**

_Genestealers on the hunt combine malevolent cunning and inhuman agility to strike and fade again and again, until their prey are reduced to panicked disarray._

At the end of your opponent's Fight phase, friendly **unengaged** **`PURESTRAIN GENESTEALER`** units can be placed in **strategic reserves** .

This **detachment** has the **`PURESTRAIN`** tag and cannot be taken with another **`PURESTRAIN` detachment** .

###### **`ENHANCEMENTS`**

##### **MARK OF THE STAR CHILDREN**<sup>~~UPGRADE~~</sup>

_Spontaneous biomorphs amongst this brood of Purestrains render them bigger, stronger, and mark them out as surely being the chosen of the Star Children themselves._

**`PURESTRAIN GENESTEALERS`** unit only. This unit has: - +1 **T** .

- 4+ **Sv** . - This unit's melee attacks have +1 **S** .

##### **TALONS OF THE SIRE**

_The broodmind bond between this Patriarch and its Purestrain spawn is so strong that it can virtually wield them like its own vicious talons._

**`PATRIARCH`** model only. This unit's attacks can re-roll **wound rolls** of 1.
