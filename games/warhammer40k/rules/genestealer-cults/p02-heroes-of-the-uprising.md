###### **`DETACHMENT RULES`**

##### **KILLER REPUTATION**

_What passes for folk heroism amongst the deviants of the Genestealer Cults is part broodmind compulsion and unwilling worship, and part these champions' ability to commit deeds of great and terrible violence against any who stand in the way of the cult._

- Friendly **`KELERMORPH/LOCUS/REDUCTUS SABOTEUR/SANCTUS`** models have **`KILLER`** .

- Friendly **`KILLER`** models' attacks can:

- Re-roll **hit rolls** of 1.

- Re-roll **wound rolls** of 1.
