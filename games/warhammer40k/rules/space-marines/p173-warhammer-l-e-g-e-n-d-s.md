# WARHAMMER L E G E N D S

###### **LAND RAIDER ACHILLES**

###### **WARHAMMER** L E G E N D S

_The Achilles is considered one of the rarest of all Land Raider variants, each one individually wrought under the scrupulous gaze of senior adepts of the Omnissiah. The result is an assault vehicle that is exceptionally resistant to energy-based weaponry. Roaring forward with impunity, the Achilles uses its thunderfire cannon and multi-meltas to suppress a wide range of foes._

###### **WARGEAR OPTIONS**

- This model's 2 twin volkite culverins can be replaced with 2 twin multi-meltas

- This model can be equipped with 1 hunter-killer missile

- This model can be equipped with 1 storm bolter

KEYWORDS: **Vehicle, Transport, Smoke, Frame, Imperium, Land Raider Achilles**
