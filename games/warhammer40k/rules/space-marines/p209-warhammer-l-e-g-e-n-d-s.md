# WARHAMMER L E G E N D S

###### **FALCHION**

###### **WARHAMMER** L E G E N D S

_The Falchion was developed to arm the Legiones Astartes with a superlative tank destroyer, and the apocalyptic power of its twin volcano cannon soon became a thing of legend. True to its name, the volcano cannon can turn rock and metal into fiery magma, and a direct hit from the weapon can be fatal to even titanic war machines._

###### **WARGEAR OPTIONS**

- This model's 2 quad lascannons can be replaced with 2 laser destroyers

- This model's twin heavy bolter can be replaced with 1 twin heavy flamer

- This model can be equipped with one of the following:

- 1 heavy bolter

- 1 heavy flamer

- 1 multi-melta

- 1 storm bolter

KEYWORDS: **Vehicle, Frame, Titanic, Imperium, Smoke, Falchion**
