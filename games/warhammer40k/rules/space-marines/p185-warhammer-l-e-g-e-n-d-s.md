# WARHAMMER L E G E N D S

###### **RAPIER CARRIER**

###### **WARHAMMER** L E G E N D S

_The Rapier Armoured Carrier is a bulky, tracked device that traces its origin to the dawn of Mankind's stellar empire. Compatible with various heavy weapons, the Rapier is most commonly fitted with a powerful quad lascannon known as a laser destroyer, making it a compact but potent anti-armour asset._

###### **WARGEAR OPTIONS**

- This model's quad heavy bolter can be replaced with one of the following:

- 1 graviton cannon

- 1 laser destroyer

- 1 quad launcher
