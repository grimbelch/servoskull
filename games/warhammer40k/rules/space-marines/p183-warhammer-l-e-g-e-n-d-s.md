# WARHAMMER L E G E N D S

###### **SICARAN OMEGA**

###### **WARHAMMER** L E G E N D S

_Retrofitted to allow the Sicaran hull to carry heavier firepower, the Omega is a formidable tank-hunter with countless victories to its name. The design utilises the speed provided by the Sicaran's engines to apply overwhelming short-range firepower in battle, racing forwards to ambush and obliterate the lumbering siege tanks of enemy columns._

###### **WARGEAR OPTIONS**

- This model can be equipped with one of the following:

- 2 heavy bolters

- 2 lascannons

- This model can be equipped with 1 hunter-killer missile

- This model can be equipped with 1 storm bolter

KEYWORDS: **Vehicle, Frame, Imperium, Smoke, Sicaran Omega**
