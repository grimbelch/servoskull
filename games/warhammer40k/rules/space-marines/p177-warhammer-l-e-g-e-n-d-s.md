# WARHAMMER L E G E N D S

###### **SICARAN ARCUS**

###### **WARHAMMER** L E G E N D S

_The Sicaran Arcus was originally conceived to supply small, unsupported Astartes forces with highly specialised weapon systems, and combines the powerful Sicaran chassis with an Arcus launcher capable of firing a wide range of warheads. Despite the mysterious censure of the Adeptus Mechanicus, surviving examples of the tank continue to serve in battle with great honour._

###### **WARGEAR OPTIONS**

- This model can be equipped with one of the following:

- 2 heavy bolters

- 2 lascannons

- This model can be equipped with 1 hunter-killer missile

- This model can be equipped with 1 storm bolter

KEYWORDS: **Vehicle, Frame, Imperium, Smoke, Sicaran Arcus**
