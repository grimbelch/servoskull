# WARHAMMER L E G E N D S

###### **LAND RAIDER PROTEUS**

###### **WARHAMMER** L E G E N D S

_The ancient and venerable Land Raider has served the Space Marines since their earliest beginnings, and the oldest variants are known as Proteus patterns. These heavily armoured bastions once acted as forward assault vehicles in Explorator missions, and rare survivals still serve the Imperium as relic vehicles of highest regard._

###### **WARGEAR OPTIONS**

- This model can be equipped with one of the following:

- 1 heavy bolter

- 1 multi-melta

- 1 twin heavy bolter

- 1 twin heavy flamer

- This model can be equipped with 1 hunter-killer missile

- This model can be equipped with 1 storm bolter

- This model can be equipped with 1 explorator augury web

KEYWORDS: **Vehicle, Transport, Smoke, Frame, Imperium, Land Raider Proteus**
