# WARHAMMER L E G E N D S

###### **TYPHON**

###### **WARHAMMER** L E G E N D S

_Prior to the creation of the Typhon, the dreadhammer siege cannon had only been utilised on static super-heavy ordnance used to pound cities to dust. Mounting this mighty weapon on a tank created a mobile and heavily armoured fortress-breaker that remains unmatched by any other relics in Space Marine armouries._
