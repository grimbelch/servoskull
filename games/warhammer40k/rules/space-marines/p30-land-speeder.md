# LAND SPEEDER

**Profile (stats):**
- M 14", T 8, SV 3+, W 9, LD 6+, OC 3

_Streaking over the battlefield on humming anti-grav engines, the Land Speeder performs blistering attack runs to rake the enemy with shots then darts away before the foe can respond. It is a valuable rapid reconnaissance asset for Space Marine forces in the field and excels in providing highly mobile fire support._
