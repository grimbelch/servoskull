# WARHAMMER L E G E N D S

###### **SICARAN BATTLE TANK**

###### **WARHAMMER** L E G E N D S

_The Sicaran is one of the rarest relic tanks seen on the battlefields of the 41st Millennium. Its unique design dates back to the Great Crusade. The most potent of the Sicaran's distinctive systems is the twin accelerator cannon, whose accuracy and rate of fire are far in excess of other weapons of its class._

###### **WARGEAR OPTIONS**

- This model can be equipped with one of the following:

- 2 heavy bolters

- 2 lascannons

- This model can be equipped with 1 hunter-killer missile

- This model can be equipped with 1 storm bolter
