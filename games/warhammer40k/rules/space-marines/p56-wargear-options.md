# WARGEAR OPTIONS

###### **ASTRAEUS**

_The Astraeus is a titanic gravitic tank armed with formidable weaponry. The most deadly of these is the twin macro-accelerator cannon, capable of unleashing high-calibre ferro-carbide slugs that can shred tanks, aircraft and ground troops. Meanwhile, its void shields can shrug off even the most concerted enemy retaliations._

###### **WARGEAR OPTIONS**

- This model's 2 Astraeus las-rippers can be replaced with 2 plasma eradicators

- This model's twin heavy bolter can be replaced with 1 twin lascannon

- This model can be equipped with 1 ironhail heavy stubber

KEYWORDS: **Vehicle, Titanic, Frame, Imperium, Astraeus**
