# ERADICATOR SQUAD WITH HEAVY BOLTERS

**Profile (stats):**
- M 5", T 6, SV 3+, W 3, LD 6+, OC 1

Standing firm, they return fire with their brutal heavy bolters, scything down enemy infantry and blowing apart the foe's light armoured vehicles with well-placed shots to weak spots in their targets' hulls._
