# WARHAMMER L E G E N D S

###### **CERBERUS**

###### **WARHAMMER** L E G E N D S

_The primary weapon of the Cerberus is the neutron pulse array, whose systems pre-date even the Great Crusade. Powered by an atomantic arc-reactor, this enormous anti-tank gun fires a pulsed beam of intense radiation that scythes straight through even the thickest armour and wreaks havoc on delicate systems within._
