# WARGEAR OPTIONS

###### **THUNDERHAWK GUNSHIP**

_Thunderhawk Gunships have served the Space Marines with distinction since the Great Crusade, combining the roles of orbital troop lander, heavy gunship and medium bomber. Thunderhawks are formidably armed for their size, with a main gun derived from frigate-class warships and a host of additional weapons._

###### **WARGEAR OPTIONS**

- This model's Thunderhawk heavy cannon can be replaced with 1 turbo-laser destructor

- This model's Thunderhawk cluster bombs can be replaced with 1 hellstrike missile battery

KEYWORDS: **Vehicle, Titanic, Fly, Aircraft, Transport, Imperium, Thunderhawk Gunship**
