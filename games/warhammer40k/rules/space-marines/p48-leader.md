# LEADER

###### **VULKAN HE'STAN**

_Striding into battle with the weapons of his Primarch in his hands, the Forgefather lays low all who oppose him. Seeker of the lost relics of Vulkan, He'stan is relentless in his quest, willing to fight through any foe and face down any danger in order to see his oaths fulfilled._

###### **LEADER**

###### **UNIT COMPOSITION**

- **This model can be attached to the following units: Assault Intercessor Squad, Company Heroes,**

- **Infernus Squad, Tactical Squad**

- **1 Vulkan He'stan - Epic Hero**

**This model is equipped with:** 1 bolt pistol; 1 Gauntlet of the Forge; 1 Spear of Vulkan

###### **INSPIRING COMMANDER**

If you include this model in your army, until the end of the battle, non- **Character** models in **Infernus Squad** units from your army have an Objective Control characteristic of 2 while they are not Battle-shocked

KEYWORDS: **Infantry, Character, Epic Hero, Grenades, Imperium, Tacticus, Captain, Vulkan He'stan**
