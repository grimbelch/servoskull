# DROP POD

###### **DROP POD**

###### **UNIT COMPOSITION**

- **1 Drop Pod**

###### **TRANSPORT**

This model has a transport capacity of 12 **Adeptus Astartes Infantry** models It cannot transport **Centurion** , **Gravis** , **Jump Pack** , **Wulfen** or **Terminator** models
