# WARHAMMER L E G E N D S

### **VETERAN BIKE SQUAD**

###### **WARHAMMER** L E G E N D S

_Matching their speed, strength and firepower against the swiftest xenos, Veteran Bikers are expert hunters. Undertaking extended missions in dangerous territory, they assess their enemy's every weakness, close off escape routes and use their velocity to enact sudden strikes from unexpected angles._

###### **WARGEAR OPTIONS**

- The Veteran Biker Sergeant can be equipped with one of the following:

- 1 Long Vigil ranged weapon

- 1 Long Vigil melee weapon

- 1 xenophase blade

- 1 Astartes shield

- Any number of Veteran Biker models can each be equipped with 1 Long Vigil melee weapon

KEYWORDS: **Mounted, Grenades, Imperium, Ordo Xenos, Retinue, Deathwatch, Veteran Bike Squad**
