# WARHAMMER L E G E N D S

#### **BARRACUDA**

###### **WARHAMMER** L E G E N D S

_The Barracuda was developed to arm the T'au Empire with a multirole fighter capable of combating the increasingly diverse threats to the Greater Good. Armed with an ion cannon and drone-controlled burst cannons, the Barracuda's versatility and damage output place it at the forefront of T'au aerial warfare._

###### **WARGEAR OPTIONS**

- This model's 2 long-barrelled burst cannons can be replaced with 2 cyclic ion blasters

- This model's swiftstrike burst cannon can be replaced with one of the following:

- 1 ion cannon

- 1 swiftstrike railgun

- This model can be equipped with up to 4 seeker missiles
