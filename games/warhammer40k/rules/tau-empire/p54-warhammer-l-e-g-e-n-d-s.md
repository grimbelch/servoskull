# WARHAMMER L E G E N D S

#### **TETRAS**

###### **WARHAMMER** L E G E N D S

_The Tetra is a lightweight and fast scout speeder used by Pathfinders for long-range infiltration, reconnaissance and sabotage missions. With limited armour and weaponry, they are not true combat vehicles, but their stealth and speed extend the operational range of Pathfinder teams, as do their surveillance and communications technologies._
