###### **`DETACHMENT RULES`**

###### **CONTRACTED HARVEST**

_Directed to inflict as much suffering as possible amongst the enemy's soldiers - on pain of their own torment - a kabal's vicious warriors spare no opportunity to unleash deluges of painful firepower._

Friendly **`BLADES FOR HIRE/KABAL`** units' ranged attacks have **`[SUSTAINED HITS 1:` non-** **`MONSTER/VEHICLE]`** .

This **detachment** has the **`KABAL`** tag and cannot be taken with another **`KABAL` detachment** .
