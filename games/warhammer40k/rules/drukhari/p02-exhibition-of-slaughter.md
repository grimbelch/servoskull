###### **`DETACHMENT RULES`**

###### **EXACTING CRUELTY**

_With long lives spent honing their murderous skills against the most powerful opponents, the renowned fighters of Commorragh's arenas have an instinct for just where to strike to bring their prey low in the most painful and elegant manner possible._

Friendly **`WYCH CULT`** units' melee attacks have **`[LETHAL HITS:` non-** **`MONSTER/VEHICLE]`** .

This **detachment** has the **`WYCH CULT`** tag and cannot be taken with another **`WYCH CULT` detachment** .

###### **`ENHANCEMENTS`**

###### **PERIAPT OF TORMENTS**

_This hollow black stone is worn as warrior jewellery. The sinister organism that lurks within it can detect those who intend malice to its bearer and punishes them by lashing their psyches with its psi-barbs._

**`SUCCUBUS`** model only. Enemy units cannot target this unit with **snap shooting** attacks.

###### **HYPERSTIMM TRAFFICKER**

_This gladiator has eyes only on the patronage of their audience. Supplied by a network of narcotic distributors - and heedless of the brutal effects of overdosing - the Succubus and their Wyches can endure long enough to provide extensive and heightened performances._
