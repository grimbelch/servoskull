#### **URIEN RAKARTH**

###### **WARHAMMER** L E G E N D S

_Urien Rakarth is the most ingenious of the many Haemonculi masters. He is the Sculptor of Torments, whose repugnant creations strain his enemies' sanity. Once his casket of fanged spirits and his toxin-laced blades are finished, a coiling nest of Haemovores feast upon whatever is left of Rakarth's foes._
