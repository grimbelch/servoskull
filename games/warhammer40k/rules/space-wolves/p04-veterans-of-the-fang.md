###### **`DETACHMENT RULES`**

###### **OLD GREYMANES**

_Even in the midst of a brutal gunfight, Grey Hunters remain cool-headed and determined to discharge their duty to Chapter and Allfather._

- When a friendly **`GREY HUNTER`** unit **starts an action** , that **action** does not prevent this unit from being **eligible to shoot** .

- In the Declare Battle Formations step, you can split a friendly **`GREY HUNTERS`** unit into two units, each with a **starting strength** of 5.

**Restrictions:** Your army can include **`SPACE WOLVES`** units, but it cannot include any **`ADEPTUS ASTARTES`** units drawn from any other Chapter.
