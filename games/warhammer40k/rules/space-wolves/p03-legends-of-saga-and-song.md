###### **`DETACHMENT RULES`**

###### **LOPING CHARGE**

_Despite the massive bulk of their armour, these elite warriors close with their enemies in a ground-eating lope that catches the enemy unprepared._

Friendly **`ADEPTUS ASTARTES TERMINATOR`** units have +1 to **charge rolls** .
