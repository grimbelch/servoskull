# WARGEAR OPTIONS

### **WARLORD TITAN**

_Warlord Titans are gargantuan embodiments of the Omnissiah's wrath. With the destructive power of a small void warship and the resilience of a fortress, Warlords reign supreme over almost all adversaries, dispensing doom with every thundering weapons salvo and crushing any survivors beneath their earth-shattering stride._
