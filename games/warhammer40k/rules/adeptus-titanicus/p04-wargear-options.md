# WARGEAR OPTIONS

### **WARHOUND TITAN**

_Warhounds are the eyes and ears of the Titan Legions, used to perform dangerous reconnaissance or raiding missions. Despite being the smallest Titan class, a single Warhound can easily turn the tide of battle when used to support ground forces, the sheer power of its weaponry and armour being more than most foes can overcome._

###### **WARGEAR OPTIONS**

- This model's Warhound plasma blastgun can be replaced with one of the following:

- 1 Warhound inferno gun

- 1 Warhound turbo-laser destructor

- 1 Warhound vulcan mega-bolter

- This model's Warhound vulcan mega-bolter can be replaced with one of the following:

- 1 Warhound inferno gun

- 1 Warhound plasma blastgun

- 1 Warhound turbo-laser destructor
