# WARGEAR OPTIONS

#### **WAR DOG MOIRAX**

_Moirax-pattern Armigers excel in speeding ahead of their larger brethren, hunting down targets of opportunity and cutting down the vulnerable. They relish the slaughter of the weak, each slain only proving the War Dogs' superiority as well as that of the monstrous deity or deities they have elected to follow in the Emperor's stead._
