**Q:** _If I am using the Auric Champions Detachment and an_ **`ADEPTUS CUSTODES CHARACTER`** _model that is leading a unit from my army is destroyed (e.g. by an attack with the_ **`[PRECISION]`** _ability), does the Assemblage of Might Detachment rule still apply to the remaining models in that unit until the start of my next Command phase?_ **A:** No.
