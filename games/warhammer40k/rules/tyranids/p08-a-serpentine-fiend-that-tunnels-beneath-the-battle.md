# A serpentine fiend that tunnels beneath the battlefield to strike

#### **THE RED TERROR**

_A serpentine fiend that tunnels beneath the battlefield to strike at unsuspecting prey, the Red Terror is a rare bioform spawned to sow disruption deep behind enemy lines. Able to distend its jaws to swallow prey whole, the beast is further able to rapidly absorb their biomass to regenerate its injuries._
