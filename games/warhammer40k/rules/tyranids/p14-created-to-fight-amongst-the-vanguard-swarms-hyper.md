# Created to fight amongst the vanguard swarms, Hyperadapted Raveners

#### **HYPERADAPTED RAVENERS**

_Created to fight amongst the vanguard swarms, Hyperadapted Raveners boast additional biomorphs and weapon symbiotes. The toxic scythes, rending talons and crushing claws of the heavier bioforms, coupled with bioacids and unnatural senses, allow them to pick off the armoured vehicles of the prey._

###### **LEADER**

This unit can be attached to the following units: **Raveners**

###### **UNIT COMPOSITION**

- **1 Ravener Prime**

- **4 Raveners**

**The Ravener Prime is equipped with:** Prime claws and talons

- **3 Raveners are equipped with:** Ravener heavy claws and talons

- **1 Ravener is equipped with:** venom bolt; Ravener heavy claws and talons
