# A living beacon of the Hive Mind's will, the Tyranid Prime

#### **TYRANID PRIME WITH LASH WHIP**

_A living beacon of the Hive Mind's will, the Tyranid Prime advances amidst swarms of warrior organisms, driving them ever forward into the prey. Should some defiant enemy anchor the line against the onrushing hordes, the Tyranid Prime strikes with its coiling lash whip to strangle, agonise and slay._
