# DEFILER

#### **DEFILER**

_Huge Daemon Engines dripping with corruption, the Defilers used by the Death Guard are horrifically powerful whether bombarding the foe from range with grotesquely diseased projectiles or tearing them apart at close quarters with piston-driven claws of rusted metal etched with cursed runes of corrosion._

###### **WARGEAR OPTIONS**

- This model's Hades battle cannon can be replaced with 1 ectoplasma destructor

- This model's excruciator cannons can be replaced with 2 magma cutters

- This model's heavy baleflamer can be replaced with one of the following:

- 1 Hades lascannon

- 1 heavy reaper autocannon

- 1 electroscourge (a model cannot be equipped with more than one electroscourge)

- This model's heavy missile launcher can be replaced with one of the following:

- 1 Hades lascannon

- 1 heavy reaper autocannon

- 1 electroscourge (a model cannot be equipped with more than one electroscourge)

KEYWORDS: **Vehicle, Walker, Chaos, Nurgle, Daemon, Defiler**
