# WARHAMMER L E G E N D S

#### **CANOPTEK TOMB SENTINEL**

###### **WARHAMMER** L E G E N D S

_Both guardian and aggressor, the Tomb Sentinel is a heavily modified variant of the Tomb Stalker, which forgoes that device's close-combat ability in favour of an exile cannon. This ancient weapon casts its target into other dimensional realms beyond the material universe, removing trespassers and upstarts as though they never existed._
