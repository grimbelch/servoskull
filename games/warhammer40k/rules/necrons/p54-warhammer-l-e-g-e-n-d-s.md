# WARHAMMER L E G E N D S

#### **CANOPTEK ACANTHRITES**

###### **WARHAMMER** L E G E N D S

_Canoptek Acanthrites are created for a single purpose: the destruction of those who have dared to claim the ancient empire of the Necrontyr. Acanthrites' articulated bodies are armed with hyper-phased voidblades and high-powered thermal cutting beams, enabling them to carve through and dissemble solid matter as though it were not there._
