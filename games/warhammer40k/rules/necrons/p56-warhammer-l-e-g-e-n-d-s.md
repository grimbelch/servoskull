# WARHAMMER L E G E N D S

#### **TESSERACT ARK**

###### **WARHAMMER** L E G E N D S

_Tesseract Arks are rare war machines of immense power, containing a singularity chamber that holds in stasis a fragment of a dying star. Siphoning off this cosmic energy, the Tesseract Ark can unleash terrible destruction, blasting foes with solar fire or excoriating particle winds, all while shielding itself within a field of gravitational distortion._
