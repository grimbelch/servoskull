#### **SERAPTEK HEAVY CONSTRUCT**

Despite its bulk, the Seraptek is an agile machine, exerting dominance over nearby Canoptek-class constructs. What purpose this colossus was originally intended for is unknown, but as a weapon of war it is truly devastating._
