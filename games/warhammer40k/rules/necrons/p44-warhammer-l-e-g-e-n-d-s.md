# WARHAMMER L E G E N D S

#### **VARGARD OBYRON**

###### **WARHAMMER** L E G E N D S

_Obyron is Nemesor Zahndrekh's devoted bodyguard. He is a tremendously skilled close-quarters combatant. Moreover, thanks to his ghostwalk mantle, he is always ready to teleport back to his master's side and place his resilient living metal form between Zahndrekh and harm, striking the foe down before they can react._
