###### **`DETACHMENT RULES`**

##### **ENTITLED TO VICTORY**

_So certain are the Flawless Blades in their arrogant pre-eminence, so sure they have already won before the first blow is struck, that they are filled with swift assurance that no foe can match._

Friendly **`FLAWLESS BLADES`** units have **Fights First** .

##### **`HONOUR IS FOR FOOLS 1CP`**
