### The Rage Unending

[RAW ✔️](/docs/publication-index/rules-index) *Apocrypha Necromunda: The Coveted Cache (2024), p7*

*This ancient boltgun is carved with so many tally marks that its plasteel case appears serrated – yet there is always
space for more. Once the bearer of the Rage Unending fires their first few bolts into pliant flesh, they will find no
greater joy than adding to that tally until their fingers cease to bend.*

| Profile Rg S Rg L Ac S Ac L S AP D Am Traits AL Cost |
| --- |
| The Rage Unending 12" 24" +1 - 4 -1 2 6+ Rapid Fire (1), Scarce, Whispering I13^ 135 |

After wounding a target with The Rage Unending for the first time, roll a D6 for every visible fighter, both friend
and foe, within 3" of the original target. On a result of a 3+, immediately resolve an additional Shoot (Basic) action
against that fighter. These attacks are resolved in an order of the controlling player's choice. If this weapon gains
the Out of Ammo condition then no more additional Shoot (Basic) actions are performed.

^ [Pilgrim's Grave Black Market](/docs/arbitrator-tools/apocrypha-optional-rules/the-coveted-cache#pilgrims-grave-black-market) only
