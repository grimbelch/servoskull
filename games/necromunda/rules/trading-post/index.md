# Trading Post

NecroRAW

The Trading Post represents the diverse markets, traders, and caravans where gangs
can barter for a wide range of weapons and equipment. While the Fighters' Equipment
Lists offer the standard gear commonly used by each fighter - the type of weapons
and armour they can easily access - the Trading Post allows them to expand their
arsenal beyond the ordinary.

See the additional [note at the bottom of this page](#necroraw-trading-post).

NecroRAW: Nomad Trading Post

Please note that Ash Waste Nomads do not have access to the standard Trading Post.
They must use the [Nomad Trading Post](/docs/nomad-trading-post) instead.

## NecroRAW: Trading Post

Unlike the *Necromunda: Core Rulebook (2023)* Trading Post, this section provides a comprehensive list of all
items in Necromunda from the Trading Post, House equipment lists, and other sources.

Each page is divided into two subsections:

- **Core Trading Post:** These are the weapons listed in the Trading Post of the *Necromunda: Core Rulebook (2023)*
  and are accessible according to the standard rules.
- **Exclusive & Trading Post Appendices:** These weapons are either exclusive to certain gangs or linked to a
  specific campaign or setting.

For items originating from books with a Trading Post Appendix, check with your Arbitrator to see if they are
available to your gang, as they are sometimes restricted to specific campaigns.

**Note:** For weapons exclusive to a specific model, their profiles are only displayed on that model's card.

### Notations on Weapons and Weapon accessories Names

- Weapons marked with an asterisk (\*) after their name take up the space of two weapons.
- Weapon accessories with a dagger (†) after their name may not be combined together on the same weapon.

### Availability and Cost

Items available at the Trading Post are marked with their Availability Level (AL), such
as C (Common), R9 (Rarity 9), or I13 (Illegal 13).

#### Exclusive Items (E)

The availability of exclusive items is indicated with an 'E'. Please note that these exclusive items cannot be
purchased through the Trading Post and are only available within the gang's Equipment Lists.

#### Cost Adjustments: Diamond (♦)

If an item is available to a fighter through their equipment list at a different cost than
in the Trading Post, it will be marked accordingly. Fighters cannot purchase such items
from the Trading Post and must instead use their own equipment list.

Any differences in cost are shown for reference only, as House Equipment Lists no longer
exist – all equipment access and costs are now determined by individual fighter equipment lists.
Consult your Fighter's Equipment list during gang creation to cross-reference the item's cost.

If an item has been made available through a specific Trading Post Appendix, its origin will
be marked accordingly, even if it has later been added as a reference in other publications.
Discuss with your arbitrator if these are available in your campaign.
