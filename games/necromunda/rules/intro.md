# Introduction to NecroRAW

**NecroRAW is an up-to-date and comprehensive platform for providing verbatim rules for Necromunda.**

The goal of NecroRAW is to serve as a reference for verbatim rules, providing
convenience for players and arbitrators to access the exact wording of rules from the books and errata.

Maintaining an online platform with verbatim rules is crucial for both new players,
who often struggle with the scattered rule distribution across various books, and
for regular players during games who need quick access to rule details.

## No House Rules

House Rules

No house rules anywhere, only RAW.

NecroRAW maintains a neutral stance by refraining from editorialising or implementing house rules to try to 'fix' the game.

Designer's Note: The Golden Rule

[RAW ✔️](/docs/publication-index/rules-index) *Necromunda: Underhive, Rulebook (2017), p39*

Necromunda is a game with lots of moving parts, and it's inevitable that rules
might sometimes come into conflict. When it's not clear how to proceed, both
players should discuss what they think is the most sensible solution - and if
an agreement cannot
be reached, roll off to decide. The most important thing is not
let debates get in the way of a fun game!

## Player-friendly (aka mobile-friendly)

NecroRAW aims to ensure that Necromunda rules are readily available to players,
especially newcomers who may struggle to navigate the fragmented distribution
of rules. By providing verbatim rules in a mobile-friendly format,
NecroRAW serves as a valuable resource for players to quickly access the
information they need during games.

## Respect for Intellectual Property

While NecroRAW prioritises accessibility, it also respects the intellectual property
rights of Games Workshop. By omitting lore and artwork from its platform, NecroRAW
seeks to strike a balance between providing essential rule access and encouraging
support for the official products. "BUY THE BOOKS!"

It's worth noting the ongoing issue with the release of rules by Games Workshop,
where the same rules are repeatedly issued across publications, leading to inflated
book sizes and a daunting barrier to entry for new players in an already niche
game compared to more established licences.

## Fork of Necro-Vox

NecroRAW started as a fork of Necro-Vox.

- The author of Necro-Vox, servo\_scribe, mentioned that approximately 15% of Necro-Vox's
  content was RAW, while the remaining 85% was RAI, either by themselves or sourced from
  Necrodamus. After receiving a takedown request, the RAW content was rewritten using AI.
- NecroRAW is based on the state of Necro-Vox before the content was rewritten. Since
  then, the 85% of RAI content has been replaced with RAW, and the lore and artwork have
  been removed. While the platform remains the same, the website has also undergone
  a style revision to make it responsive and, therefore, mobile-friendly.
- [NecroRAW's Launch Announcement](/blog/v1.0-necroraw).

**NecroRAW is now 100% RAW.**
