# Campaigns

NecroRAW

Campaigns centre around gangs fighting battles for control of various resources,
such as Territories, Road Sections, or Sympathisers. Most battles in a campaign are
fought over one of these resources, which serve as the stakes. The outcome of the
battle determines control over the resource. Alternatively, instead of resources,
the stake may be Captives held by the opposing gang.
