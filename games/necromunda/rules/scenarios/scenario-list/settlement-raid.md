# 87. Settlement Raid

[RAW ✔️](/docs/publication-index/rules-index) *Book of the Outcast, p88*

NecroRAW

Do not confuse this Outlander scenario with the Ash Wastes scenario also titled
"[Settlement Raid](/docs/scenarios/scenario-list/settlement-raid-v2)" published in *Necromunda: Core Rulebook (2023)* and *Necromunda: Ash Wastes Rulebook*.

> *Man the walls!*

In the Settlement Raid scenario, one gang launches a full-frontal assault
on another gang's settlement in order to try to loot it.

## Battlefield

The defender should set up the terrain on one half of
the battlefield to represent their settlement, placing any
defences they have (see below) in this area. The rest
of the battlefield can be set up using the guidelines
described in the Necromunda Rulebook.

## Attacker and Defender

In this scenario, one gang is the attacker and the other
is the defender. The player whose gang has the higher
rating is the attacker. If both gangs have the same
rating, players roll off and the winner decides whether
they will attack or defend. If this scenario is being
played as part of the Outlander Campaign then the gang
who made the challenge is the attacker.

## Crews

This scenario uses the standard rules for choosing a
crew, as described in the Battlefield Set-up & Scenarios
section of the Necromunda Rulebook. The attacker uses
the Custom Selection (10) method to choose their crew.
The defender uses the Random Selection (10) method
to determine their crew.

## Deployment

The defender deploys first, placing their fighters
anywhere in the half of the battlefield representing their
settlement. The attacker then deploys their entire crew
within 2" of the opposite battlefield edge.

## Reinforcements

The defender can use Reinforcements as described in
the Necromunda Rulebook. Starting on the third round,
and each round thereafter, up to two random fighters
will arrive in the End phase.

## Gang Tactics

Each player may choose two gang tactics from those
available to their gang. If, during the pre-battle
sequence, the total Credits value of fighters in one
player's starting crew is less than their opponent's, then
they may determine one additional gang tactic for each
full 100 credits of difference.

## Ending the Battle

The battle ends when only one gang has fighters
remaining on the battlefield.

## Victory

The attacker wins if they loot at least 100 credits from
the defender's settlement, otherwise the defender is
the winner.

## Rewards

#### Credits

The attacker adds D3x10 credits to their Stash for each
fighter that moved off the defender's battlefield edge.

The defender adds D6x10 credits to their Stash if they
are the winner.

#### Experience

Each fighter who took part in the battle earns 1 XP.

Each fighter who moved off the defender's battlefield
edge earns 1 XP.

#### Reputation

The victorious gang gains D3 Reputation.

If either gang bottled out, they lose 1 Reputation.

## Outlander Campaign Rewards

In an Outlander Campaign, each attacking fighter who
moves off the defender's battlefield edge earns the
attacking gang one of the following (the attacker may
choose): D6 Power, D6 Salvage or D6 Sustenance.

## Home Turf Advantage

The defender has the Home Turf Advantage (see the
Necromunda Rulebook).

## Defences

If played as part of an Outlander Campaign, in addition
to getting to set up the terrain, the defender can place
any or all of their settlement defences, such as walls,
towers or mines in their half of the battlefield. For more
information on defences and their effects, see [Outlander Terrain](/docs/battlefield-set-up/outlander-terrain).

If not played as part of an Outlander Campaign, the
defender may set up a [Gang Stronghold](/docs/battlefield-set-up/outlander-terrain#gang-strongholds-in-necromunda)
in their half of the battlefield.

## Ransacking The Settlement

The attacker is trying to get fighters into the enemy
settlement to cause havoc and steal anything that is
not nailed down. If, at the start of the End phase, an
attacking fighter is in base contact with the defender's
battlefield edge (the edge opposite the
attacker's deployment zone), they can be
removed from the battlefield. Each fighter
who successfully enters the settlement
will be able to grab some loot
(see Rewards).

## Fleeing the Battlefield

If the defender voluntarily bottles out and flees the
battlefield, then all remaining attacking fighters are
assumed to have entered the settlement.
