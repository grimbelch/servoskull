# 133. Storm Battle

[RAW ✔️](/docs/publication-index/rules-index) *The Aranthian Succession – The Vaults of Temenos, p88*

> *"Come and get some, you ash-ridden son of a sheen-harpy!"*  
> - Grol 'Road-rage', Bellows Flame, House Goliath

In this scenario, a gang takes advantage of an ash storm to attack a settlement in the wastes.

## Battle Type

This scenario is an Ash Wastes battle. Vehicles and
Wargear that grant the Mounted condition can be
included in both gangs' crews.

## Battlefield

Set up the battlefield using the [Battlefield Set-up
guidelines](/docs/battlefield-set-up), as described in the Necromunda Rulebook.
There should be narrow streets and lots of structures.

## Crews

This scenario uses the standard rules for choosing
a crew, as described in the [Battlefield Set-up & Scenarios section](/docs/battlefield-set-up/scenario-rules)
of the Necromunda: Rulebook.

Both gangs use the Custom Selection (6) method to determine their crew.

## Deployment

This scenario uses the [standard rules for deployment](/docs/battlefield-set-up/scenario-rules#deployment).

## Gang Tactics

Each player may choose two gang tactics from those
available to their gang. If, during the pre-battle
sequence, the total credits value of models in one
player's starting crew is less than their opponent's,
they may randomly determine one additional gang
tactic for each full 100 credits of difference.

## Ending the Battle

The battle ends when only one gang has models
remaining on the battlefield at the start of a round.

## Victory

If only one gang has models remaining on the
battlefield at the end of the battle then they are
victorious. If neither gang has any models remaining on
the battlefield, the battle is a draw.

## Rewards

#### Credits

The victorious gang adds 2D6x10 credits to
their Stash.

The losing gang adds D6x10 credits to their Stash.

If the battle is a draw then both gangs add 2D6x10
credits to their Stash.

#### Experience

Each model who took part in the battle earns 1 XP.

The winning gang's Leader earns D3 XP even if they did
not take part in the battle.

#### Reputation

The victorious gang gains D3 Reputation. If either gang
bottled out, they lose 1 Reputation.

## Ash Storm

The battle takes place during a fierce and violent ash
storm, reducing visibility for all. Do not roll to determine
the Season when generating battlefield conditions,
instead, use the Great Storm result from the Season
of Ash:

**Great Storm:** A great ash storm descends upon
the battlefield. The rules for Pitch Black are in effect
for the battle. In addition, at the beginning of each
round randomly determine a battlefield edge. Fighters
and vehicles moving towards this edge count each
1" moved as 2", while those moving away from this
edge count each 2" moved as 1". In the End phase all
models are moved D6" directly away from this edge –
roll separately for each model.

note

## Attack On Halfway

If players wish, they may use this scenario to
represent the Ash Waste Nomads' attack on
the settlement of Halfway under the cover of
a mighty storm. In order to do this, make the
following changes:

- The defender should be a mixed force from many
  gangs featuring Cawdor Way Brethren and led by
  Ashwood Stranger.
- The attacker should be an Ash Waste
  Nomads gang.
- The Island in the Storm special rule is in effect.

### Island In The Storm

Ashwood Stranger automatically passes any Cool
checks he is called upon to make, as does any
friendly model within 6" of him.
