# 120. Market Mayhem

[RAW ✔️](/docs/publication-index/rules-index) *The Aranthian Succession – Cinderak Burning, p100*

NecroRAW

Do not confuse this scenario with the scenario also titled "[Market Mayhem](/docs/scenarios/scenario-list/market-mayhem)" from *Book of the Outcast*.

> *"Burn it all down!"*  
> - Gorshiv, Shiv's Crushers, House Goliath

In this scenario, a gang makes a last stand in the middle of a burning market.

## Attacker and Defender

In this scenario, one gang is the attacker and the other
is the defender. If this scenario is being played as part
of a campaign then the gang that issued the challenge
is the attacker, otherwise roll off with the winner
deciding whether they will attack or defend.

## Battle Type

This scenario is an Ash Wastes battle; vehicles and
Wargear that grant the Mounted condition can be
included in both gangs' starting crew.

## Battlefield

Set up the battlefield as described in the Battlefield Setup
& Scenarios section of the Necromunda Rulebook.
The area within 9" of the centre of the battlefield should
be set up as a market.

## Crews

This scenario uses the standard rules for choosing a
crew, as described in the Battlefield Set-up & Scenarios
section of the Necromunda Rulebook. Both gangs
use the Custom Selection (7) method to determine
their crews.

## Deployment

The defender deploys their starting crew first within the
market. The attacker then deploys their starting crew
within 3" of any battlefield edge.

## Gang Tactics

Each player may choose two gang tactics from those
available to their gang. If, during the pre-battle
sequence, the total credits value of fighters in one
player's starting crew is less than their opponent's, they
may randomly determine one additional gang tactic for
each full 100 credits of difference.

## Ending the Battle

The battle ends when either only one gang has fighters
remaining on the battlefield or at the end of the
fifth round.

## Victory

The gang with the most Victory points is the victor. If
both gangs have the same number of Victory points
then the result is a draw.

## Rewards

#### Credits

The victorious gang adds 2D6x10 credits to their Stash.
The losing gang adds D6x5 credits to their Stash.

If the battle ends in a draw then both gangs add D6x10
credits to their Stash.

#### Experience

Each model who took part in the battle earns 1 XP.

If a model takes a Champion or Leader Out of Action,
they earn an additional 1 XP.

#### Reputation

The victorious gang gains D3 Reputation. If either gang
bottled out, they lose 1 Reputation.

## Home Turf Advantage

The defender has the Home Turf Advantage.

## Burning Market

The entire market has been set ablaze. If a model
ends its activation within 1" of a burning structure the
controlling player rolls a D6, on a 4+ they are set on fire
and become subject to the Blaze condition. In addition,
the Visibility (6") rule is in effect due to the billowing
smoke coming from the market.

## Last Stand

At the start of each End phase, if the only attacking
models within the market are Seriously Injured fighters
or Wrecked vehicles, the defender gains 1 Victory point.
Otherwise, the attacker gains 1 Victory point.

## Fleeing the Battlefield

If either gang voluntarily bottles out and subsequently
flees the battlefield, their opponent automatically wins
the scenario.

note

## Athera's Last Stand

If players wish, they may use this scenario to
represent Athera's desperate defence of the centre
of Cinderak City before Lady Credo arrived to drive
Gorshiv and the Goliaths off. In order to do this,
make the following changes:

- The defender uses an Escher gang including
  Athera and Stix.
- The attacker uses a Goliath gang including
  Gorshiv Hammerfist.
