# 167. Death From Above

[RAW ✔️](/docs/publication-index/rules-index) *The Aranthian Succession – Spire of Primus, p82*

> *"These base-born hivers are so used to tunnels that they forget to watch the skies."*  
> - Norn Helmawr, Baron of the Underhells, Yeld Spyre Hunter, House Helmawr

In this scenario, one gang attempts to break into a hive via its landing field.

## Attacker and Defender

In this scenario, one gang is the attacker and the other
is the defender. If this scenario is being played as part
of a campaign, then the gang that issued the challenge
is the attacker; otherwise, roll off with the winner
deciding whether they will attack or defend.

## Battle Type

This scenario is an Underhive battle; vehicles and
Wargear that grant the Mounted condition cannot be
included in either gang's starting crew.

## Battlefield

This scenario uses the standard rules for setting up
a battlefield, as described in the Battlefield Set-up &
Scenarios section of the *Necromunda Core Rulebook*
with the following exception; before placing any terrain
the Defender selects a battlefield edge as the Landing
Field Edge. No terrain should be placed within 6" of the
Landing Field Edge.

## Crews

This scenario uses the standard rules for choosing a
crew, as described in the Battlefield Set-up & Scenarios
section of the *Necromunda Core Rulebook*. Both gangs
use the Custom Selection (7) method to determine
their crew.

## Deployment

The attacker sets up their starting crew in base contact
with the Landing Field Edge. The defender then deploys
their starting crew anywhere on the battlefield at least
9" away from enemy fighters.

## Gang Tactics

This scenario uses the standard rules for gang tactics
as described in the Battlefield Set-up & Scenarios
section of the *Necromunda Core Rulebook*.

## Ending the Battle

If either gang has no fighters left on the battlefield at
the end of any round, the battle ends immediately.

## Victory

If at least three of the attacker's fighters Broke Through
(see Breaking Into Primus opposite) they are victorious.
Otherwise, the defender is victorious.

## Rewards

#### Credits

The victorious gang adds 2D6x10 credits to their
Stash.

The losing gang adds D6x10 credits to their Stash.

#### Experience

Any fighter that successfully Breaks Through earns an
additional 1 XP.

Any fighter that takes an enemy fighter Out of Action
using the Shove (Basic) action earns an additional
1 XP.

#### Reputation

The victorious gang gains D3 Reputation.

## Home Turf Advantage

The defender has the Home Turf Advantage as
described in the Battlefield Set-up & Scenarios section
of the *Necromunda Core Rulebook*.

## Breaking into Primus

The battlefield edge opposite the Landing Field
Edge is considered the Exit Edge. At the start of
each End phase any attacking fighters within 1"
of the Exit Edge may Break Through. Remove the
fighter from the battlefield. They are considered
to have been taken Out of Action for the purposes
of Bottle checks.

## Access Denied

Defending fighters can try to push attacking fighters
over the Landing Field Edge if they are within 3" of it
by performing the Shove (Basic) action.

**Shove (Basic):** The target must make an Initiative
test. If the test is failed, they immediately topple from
the edge of the Landing Field and go Out of Action.

## Hive Breach

The air is being sucked out, making it difficult for
fighters to maintain their positions. If a fighter is
standing at the end of their activation, that fighter
makes a Strength check. If failed, they become Prone
and Pinned.

## Fleeing the Battlefield

If either gang voluntarily bottles out and subsequently
flees the battlefield, their opponent automatically wins
the scenario.

note

## Haera's Strike Force

If players wish, they may use this scenario to
represent Haera and her Spyrers breaking back into
Hive City and smashing through the Enforcers'
blockade. In order to do so, make the following
changes:

- The attacker should be a Spyre Hunting Party gang
  led by Lady Haera in Sthenian-pattern Hunting Rig.
- The defender should be a Palanite Enforcers gang.
- The defending gang rolls a D6 before the start of
  the battle, before the first roll for Priority. Pick that
  many Enforcers at random. Each one starts the
  battle with a Flesh Wound.
