# 161. Explore the Depths

[RAW ✔️](/docs/publication-index/rules-index) *Halls of the Ancients, p106*

Ironhead Squat Scenarios

The following scenarios are designed to reflect some of the tactics Ironhead Squat
prospectors might employ or common situations they might find themselves in.
They include fighting off others from a valuable wasteland claim they have found
and delving into the depths in search of ancient technology to enrich the clan.

When an Ironhead Squat player has the option of choosing the scenario for their
battle, they can, with the permission of the Arbitrator, choose one of the following
scenarios. Where one player is the attacker and the other is the defender,
this will be detailed in the scenario.

Designer's Note: Challenge Accepted!

While the scenarios presented here have been designed specifically for Ironhead
Squat prospectors, there is no reason that, with the permission of the Arbitrator,
they might not be used for any gang. Both scenarios also lend themselves well to
multi-player scenarios – such as the Depths Rush where multiple gangs hearing
of a newly discovered collapsed dome rush in to try to claim
its treasures for themselves.

In this scenario, two gangs chase after auspex-ghosts in an
irradiated section of the underhive in search of riches.

## Battle Type

This scenario is an Underhive battle; vehicles and
Wargear that grant the Mounted condition cannot
be included in either gang's starting crew.

## Battlefield

This scenario uses the standard rules for setting up
a battlefield, as described in the Battlefield Set-up &
Scenarios section of the Necromunda Core Rulebook.

## Crews

This scenario uses the standard rules for choosing
a crew, as described in the Battlefield Set-up &
Scenarios section of the Necromunda Core Rulebook.
Both gangs use the Hybrid Selection (4+D3)
method to determine their crew.

## Deployment

This scenario uses the standard rules for deployment
as described in the Battlefield Set-up & Scenarios
section of the Necromunda Core Rulebook.

## Gang Tactics

This scenario uses the standard rules for gang tactics
as described in the Battlefield Set-up & Scenarios
section of the Necromunda Core Rulebook.

## Ending the Battle

If either gang has no fighters left on the battlefield
or there are no Auspex-ghost markers remaining on
the battlefield at the end of any round, the battle
ends immediately.

## Victory

If one gang has more Archeotech points than their
opponent they are victorious. Any other result is a draw.

## Rewards

#### Credits

Each gang adds D6x10 credits to their Stash for each
Archeotech point they have.

#### Experience

If a fighter increases their gang's Archeotech points by
at least one, they earn D3 XP.

#### Reputation

The victorious gang gains D3 Reputation.

If either gang bottled out, they lose 1 Reputation.

## Search for Archeotech

After the battlefield is set up, before deploying starting
crews, players take it turn to place 6 Auspex-ghost
markers on the battlefield starting with the winner of a
roll-off. Each Auspex-ghost marker must be placed at
least 9" away from any other Auspex-ghost and 6" from
any battlefield edge.

Any fighter within 1" of an Auspex-ghost may perform
the Investigate Ghost (Basic) action:

**Investigate Ghost (Basic):** This fighter makes an
Intelligence test; if the test is passed they find some
Archeotech – increase the gang's Archeotech points
by one, if the test is failed the Auspex-ghost was scrap,
add D3x5 credits to the gang's Stash. Regardless of
the test result, remove the Auspex-ghost marker from
the battlefield.

## Radiation Leak

At the start of each round, after rolling for Priority
before activating any fighters, the player with Priority
rolls a D6 and consults the below table. These effects
last for the duration of the round:

|  |  |  |  |  |  |  |  |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

| D6 | Result |
| --- | --- |
| 1-3 | No effect. |
| 4-5 | Roll a D6 for every fighter on the battlefield, on a 5+ they suffer a Flesh Wound. Fighters that are immune to the Rad-phage trait are not affected. |
| 6 | Every fighter on the battlefield suffers a Flesh Wound. Fighters that are immune to the Rad-phage trait are only affected on a 5+. |

## Fleeing the Battlefield

If either gang voluntarily bottles out and subsequently
flees the battlefield, their opponent automatically
wins the scenario.
