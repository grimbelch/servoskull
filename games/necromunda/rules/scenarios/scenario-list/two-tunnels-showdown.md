# 122. Two Tunnels Showdown

[RAW ✔️](/docs/publication-index/rules-index) *Apocrypha Necromunda: Twins of Two Tunnels, p6*

> *"Waiting for the Hammer to fall? This is the Gunk Queen's territory and he can't touch it!"*  
> - Url Twice-broke, Two Tunnels Agitator

In this scenario, a gang defends its precious Gunk Tank whilst a rival gang tries to pull it down.

## Attacker and Defender

In this scenario, one gang is the attacker and the
other is the defender. In a campaign, the player who
chose this scenario is the attacker. In a skirmish,
players roll off and the winner decides whether they
will attack or defend.

## Battle Type

This scenario can be played as either a Zone Mortalis
or Sector Mechanicus battle; vehicles and wargear
that grant the Mounted condition cannot be
included in either gang's crew.

## Battlefield

Set up the battlefield as described in the Battlefield
Set-up section of the Necromunda Rulebook. Players
should place a Gunk Tank in the centre of the
battlefield.

## Crews

This scenario uses the standard rules for choosing a
crew as described in the Battlefield Set-up section
of the Necromunda Rulebook. Both gangs use the
Custom Selection (7) method.

## Deployment

The defender deploys their starting crew within 6"
of the Gunk Tank. The attacker then deploys their
starting crew within 3" of any battlefield edge.

## Gang Tactics

Each player may choose two gang tactics from those
available to their gang. If, during the pre-battle
sequence, the total credits value of fighters in one
player's starting crew is less than their opponent's,
they may randomly determine one additional gang
tactic for each full 100 credits of difference.

## Ending the Battle

The battle ends immediately if, at the end of any
round, only one gang has fighters remaining on the
battlefield or the Gunk Tank has fallen.

## Victory

If the Gunk Tank has fallen the attacker wins,
otherwise, the defender wins.

## Rewards

#### Credits

The victorious gang adds D6x10 credits to their Stash.

#### Experience

Every fighter who took part in the battle earns 1 XP.

Each time an attacking fighter successfully adds
a Toppled token to the Gunk Tank, they earn 1
additional XP.

#### Reputation

The victorious gang gains D3 Reputation.

If either gang bottled out, they lose 1 Reputation.

## Home Turf Advantage

The defender has the Home Turf Advantage.

## The Gorvos Gunk Tank

Central to the fight is the Gunk Tank, with the
attacker attempting to topple it to deprive the
defender of much needed gunk. The Gunk Tank can
be represented by any suitable large container (ideally
at least 3" in diameter) – the one found in the Zone
Mortalis: Gang Stronghold is perfect.

Any Standing and Active attacking fighter within 1"
of the Gunk Tank may make the Topple Tank (Double)
action:

**Topple Tank (Double):** The active fighter rolls a D6
and adds their Strength. If the total is 8 or higher then
the Gunk Tank starts to fall –
place a Toppled token
next to the Gunk Tank.

Once the Gunk Tank has four Toppled tokens, it
falls! All fighters within 3" of the Gunk Tank are
placed Prone and Pinned and suffer a S4, AP -, D1 hit
and the Topple Tank (Double) action may no longer
be performed.

## Fleeing the Battlefield

If either gang voluntarily bottles out and subsequently
flees the battlefield, their opponent automatically
wins the scenario.

note

## Twins of Two Tunnels

If players wish, they may use this scenario to
represent Axon Hammer's attempt to destroy the
Gunk Tank at the heart of Two Tunnels. In order
to do this, make the following changes:

- The attacker's gang should be a Goliath gang including a [Propagandist](/docs/gangs/gang-additions/hangers-on-and-brutes/hangers-on#0-1-propagandist) and [Axon Hammer](/docs/gangs/gang-additions/hired-guns/house-agents/outlaw-house-agents#axon-hammer-scourge-of-the-spider-points) using the [Irontree Reavers](/docs/gangs/gang-lists/house-goliath/gene-smithing#the-irontree-reavers) rules.
- The defender's gang should be an Underhive Outcast gang including an [Agitator](/docs/gangs/gang-additions/hangers-on-and-brutes/hangers-on#0-1-agitator) and led by [Gaen](/docs/gangs/gang-additions/hired-guns/hive-scum/outlaw-hive-scum#gaen-the-gunk-queen-gorvos) and [Vunder Gorvos](/docs/gangs/gang-additions/hired-guns/hive-scum/outlaw-hive-scum#vunder-gorvos).
- The War of Words special rule is in effect (see [Propagandist](/docs/gangs/gang-additions/hangers-on-and-brutes/hangers-on#0-1-propagandist)/[Agitator](/docs/gangs/gang-additions/hangers-on-and-brutes/hangers-on#0-1-agitator)).
