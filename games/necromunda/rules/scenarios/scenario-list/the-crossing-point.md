# 77. The Crossing Point

[RAW ✔️](/docs/publication-index/rules-index) *Necromunda: Hive War, Rulebook (2021), p80*

> *Two rival gangs meet across an underhive thoroughfare that marks the border between their territories.*

## Battlefield

Set up the battlefield as shown below.

![](/assets/images/the-crossing-point-fbdbca61189622677f4012bb09e500ac.avif)

## Crews

Both players use the Custom Selection (5) method to choose their crew.

## Deployment

This scenario uses the Stand-off deployment rules (see page 78). The
two deployment areas are shaded in red and green, respectively.

## Objectives – To the Death

Each gang's aim is simple; eliminate the opposing gang. The gang left
standing at the end of the battle is the winner.

## Ending the Battle

If either gang has no fighters left on the battlefield at the end of any
round, the battle ends immediately.

## Victory

If only one gang has fighters left on the battlefield at the end on the
battle and the other does not, that gang is automatically victorious.
If both players have no fighters left on the battlefield, the battle ends in a draw.

note

## Linking Games Together

If players wish to link their games together (as described on page 87),
the victorious gang now controls this section of the thoroughfare and is
able to launch assaults upon their rival's stronghold. The player controlling
the victorious gang is automatically the attacker at the start of the next battle.
