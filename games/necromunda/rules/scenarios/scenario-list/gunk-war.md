# 110. Gunk War

[RAW ✔️](/docs/publication-index/rules-index) *The Aranthian Succession – Cinderak Burning, p80*

> *"This may not be much, but it is mine and no mutie scum is gonna take it away fromme!"*  
> - Gaen 'The Gunk Queen' Gorvos

In this scenario, a Gunk Tank must be defended while it is drained, before an attacking gang can destroy it.

## Attacker and Defender

In this scenario, one gang is the attacker and the other
is the defender. If this scenario is being played as part
of a campaign then the gang that issued the challenge
is the attacker, otherwise roll off with the winner
deciding whether they will attack or defend.

## Battle Type

This scenario is a Sector Mechanicus battle; vehicles
and Wargear that grant the Mounted condition cannot
be included in either gangs' starting crew.

## Battlefield

Place a large object in the centre of the battlefield;
a Gunk Tank from the Gang Stronghold kit is ideal.
Mark out a ring with a 6" radius with the Gunk Tank
in the centre to represent the Ring of Fire. The rest of
the battlefield is set up using the Battlefield Set-up
guidelines, as described in the Necromunda Rulebook.

## Crews

This scenario uses the standard rules for choosing a
crew, as described in the [Battlefield Set-up & Scenarios section](/docs/battlefield-set-up/scenario-rules) of the Necromunda Rulebook.

The attacker uses the Custom Selection (6) method to choose their crew.
The defender uses the Random Selection (6) method to
determine their crew.

## Deployment

The defender deploys their starting crew first within the
Ring of Fire. The attacker then deploys their starting
crew anywhere on the battlefield outside of the Ring of
Fire and at least 6"away from it.

## Gang Tactics

Each player may choose two gang tactics from those
available to their gang. If, during the pre-battle
sequence, the total credits value of fighters in one
player's starting crew is less than their opponent's, they
may randomly determine one additional gang tactic for
each full 100 credits of difference.

## Ending the Battle

The battle ends at the end of a round when either only
one gang has fighters remaining on the battlefield, five
battle rounds have been played or if the Gunk Tank
has exploded.

## Victory

The defender wins if, at the end of the battle, the Gunk
Tank has not exploded and they have at least one
fighter left on the battlefield. Any other result is a victory
for the attacker.

## Rewards

#### Credits

The victorious gang adds 2D6x10 credits to their Stash.

The losing gang adds D3x10 credits to their Stash.

#### Experience

Each fighter who took part in the battle earns 1 XP.

Each attacking fighter that deals at least one wound to
the Gunk Tank earns an additional 1 XP.

If the defender wins, their Leader earns D3 XP even if
they did not take part in the battle.

#### Reputation

The victorious gang gains D3 Reputation. If either gang
bottled out, they lose 1 Reputation.

## Home Turf Advantage

The defender has the Home Turf Advantage.

## Ring of Fire

The Ring of Fire is filled with gunk that has been set
alight; any ranged attack that draws line of sight through
it suffers a -1 penalty to hit. Fighters are subject to the
Revealed condition while they are within 3" of the fire
trench. A fighter may cross the Ring of Fire, but any that
do suffer a S2, AP -, D1 hit as if from a weapon with the
Blaze trait (note that this hit will not cause the fighter to
be Pinned) after their move has finished.

## Bottom of the Barrel

The Gunk Tank can only be damaged by melee attacks; it
is considered to have 3 Wounds, Toughness 6 and a 4+
armour save. If it is reduced to 0 Wounds, it will explode!
Any fighter within 3" takes a S5, AP -1, D2 hit with the
Blaze trait.

## Fleeing the Battlefield

If either gang voluntarily bottles out and subsequently
flees the battlefield, their opponent automatically wins
the scenario.

note

## The Defence of Two Tunnels

If players wish they may use this scenario to represent the defence of Two Tunnels as Corpse Grinder Cults invade the settlement, attempting to consume all within it. The Gunk Queen and her brother desperately attempt to defend the industrial gunkworks by flooding the surrounding streets with gunk and setting it alight! In order to do this, make the following changes:

- The defender takes an Outcast gang which must include [Gaen 'The Gunk Queen' Gorvos](/docs/gangs/gang-additions/hired-guns/hive-scum/outlaw-hive-scum#gaen-the-gunk-queen-gorvos) as its Leader and [Vunder Gorvos](/docs/gangs/gang-additions/hired-guns/hive-scum/outlaw-hive-scum#vunder-gorvos) as a Champion.
- The attacker takes a [Corpse Grinder](/docs/gangs/gang-lists/corpse-grinder-cults/) gang.
