# 130. Convoy Raid

[RAW ✔️](/docs/publication-index/rules-index) *The Aranthian Succession – The Vaults of Temenos, p80*

> *"Remember, lads – in nice and quick, grab the cargo and get out. It'll be an easy job."*  
> - Last words of Jorgun Lark, Wheels of Fire, House Orlock

In this scenario, one gang is trying to protect a Ridgehauler from a sudden attack.

## Attacker and Defender

In this scenario, one gang is the attacker and the other is the defender. If this scenario is being played as part of a campaign, then the gang that issued the challenge is the attacker; otherwise, roll off with the winner deciding whether they will attack or defend.

## Battle Type

This scenario is a Rolling Roads battle with open sides;
vehicles and Wargear that grant the Mounted condition
can be included in both gangs' starting crew.

## Battlefield

Set up a large vehicle in the centre of the battlefield, a
Guild of Coin Ridgehauler is ideal (see Cargo Vehicle).
Mark a road 6"- 8" wide running up the centre of the
battlefield, note – no terrain can be placed on this
road. The rest of the battlefield is set up following
the [Battlefield Set-up guidelines](/docs/battlefield-set-up) , as described in the
Necromunda Rulebook.

This scenario uses the standard rules for setting up a battlefield, as described in the [Battlefield Set-up & Scenarios section](/docs/battlefield-set-up) of the Necromunda Core Rulebook.

## Crews

This scenario uses the standard rules for choosing a crew, as described in the [Battlefield Set-up & Scenarios section](/docs/battlefield-set-up/scenario-rules) of the Necromunda Rulebook.

Both gangs use the Custom Selection (7) method to choose their crew.

## Deployment

The defender deploys their starting crew anywhere
on the battlefield within 6" of the Cargo Vehicle. The
attacker then deploys their starting crew within 3" of
the Trailing Edge.

## Gang Tactics

Each player may choose two gang tactics from those
available to their gang. If, during the pre-battle
sequence, the gang rating of one player's gang is less
than their opponent's, they may randomly determine
one additional gang tactic for each full 100 credits
of difference.

## Ending the Battle

The battle ends if at the start of an End phase only one
gang has models left on the battlefield.

## Victory

If the attacker has at least 5 Loot points then they are
victorious, otherwise the defender is the victor.

## Rewards

#### Credits

The attacker adds D6x10 credits to their Stash for
each Loot point they claim.

The defender adds D3x10 credits to their Stash for
each attacking fighter taken Out of Action and each
enemy vehicle Wrecked.

#### Experience

Each model who took part in the battle earns 1 XP.

Each fighter that claims a Loot point earns an
additional 1 XP.

If the defender wins their Leader earns D3 XP even if
they did not take part in the battle.

#### Reputation

The victorious gang gains D3 Reputation. If either gang
bottled out, they lose 1 Reputation.

## Cargo Vehicle

The attacker is trying to loot what they can from a
passing vehicle while its escort tries to protect it. Place
a large vehicle in the centre of the battlefield (a Guild
of Coin Ridgehauler is ideal for this) facing directly
towards the Leading Edge. The Cargo Vehicle cannot
be targeted by attacks or moved from its position in the
centre of the battlefield. Any fighter from the attacking
gang within 1" of the Cargo Vehicle can take the Loot
Cargo (Basic) action:

**Loot Cargo (Basic):** Make an Intelligence check for
this fighter, if it is passed, increase their gang's Loot
points by 1.

## High Speed Pursuit

This scenario happens at high speed with the Cargo
Vehicle attempting to shake off the attackers. During
the Move Battlefield step of the Rolling Roads phase,
any vehicle or fighter subject to the Mounted condition,
that has at least half of the Leading Edge within its
front vision arc, is not moved towards the Trailing Edge
along with the rest of the battlefield.

This may result in those models colliding with other
models or terrain. Resolve any vehicle collisions as
described in the Ash Wastes Rulebook. If a fighter
subject to the Mounted condition that remains
stationary in this way collides with another model or
terrain, they are placed Prone and Pinned suffering a
hit as per the I Get Knocked Down… table.

## Implacable Advance

The Cargo Vehicle always remains in the centre of the
battlefield and cannot be moved, other models treat it
as a solid terrain feature.

## Clear Road

The road going through the centre of the battlefield is a
clearway and so no terrain can ever be placed upon it.

## Fleeing the Battlefield

If either gang voluntarily bottles out and subsequently
flees the battlefield, their opponent automatically wins
the scenario.

note

## AXON'S DEFENCE

If players wish, they may use this scenario to
represent Axon Hammer defending one of the
many convoys travelling across the wastes from
Lady Credo's attacks. In order to do this, make the
following changes:

- The attacking gang should be an Escher or
  Orlock gang.
- The defending gang should be a Goliath gang led
  by Axon Hammer.
