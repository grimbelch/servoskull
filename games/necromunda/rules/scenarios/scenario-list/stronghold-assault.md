# 78. Stronghold Assault

[RAW ✔️](/docs/publication-index/rules-index) *Necromunda: Hive War, Rulebook (2021), p81*

> *Seizing upon their advantage, a gang launches a deadly assault upon the territory of a rival gang.*

## Battlefield

Set up the battlefield as shown below.

![stronghold-assault.avif](/assets/images/stronghold-assault-abccdadba2ac05f3cef0a7c610a96118.avif)

## Crews

Both players use the Random Selection (5) method to choose their starting crew.

## Deployment

This scenario uses the attacker and defender deployment rules (see page 78). The
defender deploys their crew within the green deployment zone, the attacker within
the red deployment zone.

## Objectives – Seize the Gatehouse

The attacking gang is attempting to storm and seize control of the gatehouse to their
enemy's stronghold. The defender is attempting to keep them out. The gatehouse is
represented on the map by the blue shaded area.

## Ending the Battle

If either gang has no fighters left on the battlefield at the end of any round, the
battle ends immediately. The battle will also end if, at the end of the second round,
one gang has at least one fighter within the gatehouse and their opponent does not.

## Victory

If only attacker has any fighters within the gatehouse at the end on the battle, they
are victorious. Alternatively, if only one gang has fighters on the battlefield at the
end of the game, they are victorious. Otherwise, the battle ends in a draw.

note

## Linking Games Together

If players wish to link their games together (as described on page 87), the victorious
gang has either seized or retained control of the gatehouse. In either case, they will
be able to bring greater numbers to bear in the next battle. The player controlling the
victorious gang may increase their starting crew size by one at the start of the next battle.
