# 105. Assassinate

[RAW ✔️](/docs/publication-index/rules-index) *White Dwarf 476, Underdogs (05/2022), p125*

> *A key member of a rival gang has a price on their head, and the desperate Underdogs intend to collect!*

note

## The Underdog

This [scenario] makes frequent reference to an Underdog;
an Underdog is defined as a gang whose Gang Rating
is at least 400 credits fewer than its opponent's.

## Battlefield

This scenario uses the Battlefield Set-up guidelines, as
described in the Battlefield Set-up & Scenarios section of
the Necromunda Rulebook.

## Attacker and Defender

In this scenario, the Underdog is the attacker and the
other gang is the defender.

## Crews

This scenario uses the standard rules for choosing a crew,
as described in the Battlefield Set-up & Scenarios section
of the Necromunda Rulebook. Both players use the
Custom Selection method to choose their crew.

## Deployment

This scenario uses the standard rules for deployment, as
described in the Battlefield Set-up & Scenarios section of
the Necromunda Rulebook.

## Targeted for Death

A large price has been placed on one of the key members
of the defender's gang. After both sides have deployed,
the attacker secretly notes down one of the defender's
fighters with the Gang Hierarchy (X) rule to be the
Target. If there are no defending fighters with the Gang
Hierarchy (X) rule, then any defending fighter may be
chosen. As soon as the Target has either been taken Out
of Action or fled the battle or one gang has no fighters
left on the battlefield, then the identity of the Target
is revealed.

## Ending the Battle

The battle ends if either the Target is taken Out of Action,
or the Target has fled the battlefield, or if one gang has
no fighters left on the battlefield.

## Victory

The attacker wins if the Target has been taken Out of
Action or the defending gang voluntarily bottled out.
Otherwise, the defender wins.

## Rewards

#### Credits

If the Target is taken Out of Action, then the attacking
gang adds 6D6x10 credits to their Stash.

If the Target flees the battlefield, then the attacking gang
adds 4D6x10 credits to their Stash.

If the Target is Seriously Injured at the end of the
battle, then the attacking gang adds 2D6x10 credits to
their Stash.

#### Experience

Each fighter who took part in the battle earns 1 XP.

The fighter that takes the Target Out of Action earns an
additional 1 XP.

If the Target survives the battle, they earn an additional
D3 XP.

#### Reputation

The victorious gang gains D3 Reputation. If either gang
bottled out, they lose 1 Reputation.
