# Secundan Incursion Gangs

[RAW ✔️](/docs/publication-index/rules-index) *The Book of Desolation, p43*

note

## Why Play A Secundan Incursion Gang?

Many parties venture into the depths of Hive
Secundus in search of riches and archeotech.
Those that wish to survive would be wise to
join up with a Spyre Hunter of House Helmawr;
the gang gains a powerful protector able to kill
the Malstrains that infest the Underhells, while
the Spyrer gains bait to lure out and distract the
xenos so they can be slain.

This symbiotic relationship has proven beneficial
to both parties since the fall of Hive Secundus
and it is an exceptionally brave (and foolish) gang
which ventures into those dread depths without
the protection – dubious though it may be –
of a Spyre Hunter.

A Secundan Incursion gang combines the strength
of a Spyre Hunter with the flexibility of another
gang. By replacing the gang's Leader with a Spyrer,
the gang sacrifices the customisability of their
Leader in order to bring a powerful fighter that is
capable of turning battles on their own.

## Gang Composition

A Secundan Incursion gang is created using the
standard rules for gang creation (see the Necromunda
Core Rulebook) with the following exceptions:

- The base gang for a Secundan Incursion gang can
  be any of the six House gangs (Cawdor, Delaque,
  Escher, Goliath, Orlock or Van Saar), Ironhead Squat
  Prospectors, Palatine Enforcers or Venators.
  - A Secudan Incursion gang has access to all of the
    fighters, weapons, Wargear and options of their
    base gang, and is treated as the base gang in
    addition to being a Secundan Incursion gang.
- Instead of a Leader from their gang list, a Secundan
  Incursion gang uses either an [Orrus Spyre Hunter](/docs/gangs/gang-lists/spyre-hunting-party/#orrus-spyre-hunter-champion),
  [Yeld Spyre Hunter](/docs/gangs/gang-lists/spyre-hunting-party/#yeld-spyre-hunter-champion), [Malcadon Spyre Hunter](/docs/gangs/gang-lists/spyre-hunting-party/#malcadon-spyre-hunter-champion) or [Jakara
  Spyre Hunter](/docs/gangs/gang-lists/spyre-hunting-party/#jakara-spyre-hunter-champion). This fighter
  changes their fighter type to Leader and gains the
  A Leader Apart and Secundan Incursion Gang Leader
  special rules.
- Additional or replacement Spyrers cannot be hired.

note

**A Leader Apart:** A Secundan Incursion gang
must take a fighter with this special rule as their
Leader. This fighter does not benefit from the Gang
Hierarchy (X) rule and can never be included in
Group Activations.

**Secundan Incursion Gang Leader:** A fighter with
this special rule may not retire during Campaign
play. For a gang leader, the only way out is death.
Should this fighter die during the course of a
campaign, or otherwise be removed from the gang,
then when a new fighter is promoted to become
the new Leader (see Death of a Leader in the
Necromunda Core Rulebook) they change their
fighter type to the Leader of the base gang, they do
not become a Spyrer.
