# Nomad Trading Post

[RAW ✔️](/docs/publication-index/rules-index) *Tribes of the Wastelands, p96*

The following pages contain rules for all of the weapons and Wargear available to gangs and fighters through the
Nomad Trading Post.

Players should note that the Nomad Trading Post is
only available to Ash Waste Nomads gangs and cannot
be accessed by other gangs. Although some Imperial
weaponry and equipment does appear in the Nomad
Trading Post, it is often harder to obtain than usual,
the Ash Waste Nomads preferring to use their own
equipment which has been designed to survive the
harsh and unforgiving environment.

During the Visit the Trading Post sub-step of the
Update Roster step of the post-battle sequence, Ash
Waste Nomads gangs perform the Seek Scavenged
Equipment action instead of the Seek Rare & Illegal
Equipment action.

**Seek Scavenged Equipment**
**(Trade Post-battle Action Only):** This action can only
be performed if a fighter has made a Trade post-battle
action, is only performed once and is done
to determine the availability of Scavenged items on
offer at the Nomad Trading Post (see below). Roll
2D6 to determine the availability of items, adding the
following bonuses:

- +2 if the gang's Leader is making a Trade
  Post-battle action.
- +1 for each Champion making a Trade
  Post-battle action.
- +1 for every full 10 Reputation the gang has.
- +/-X as from a specified special rule.

An Ash Waste Nomads gang may purchase equipment
from the Nomad Trading Post that is Scavenge (X)
where X is equal to or less than the Availability level.

Note that Exclusive items may not be purchased from
the Trading Post, only from gang Equipment Lists.

## NecroRAW: Nomad Trading Post

### Notations on Weapons and Weapon accessories Names

- Weapons marked with an asterisk (\*) after their name take up the space of two weapons.
- Weapon accessories with a dagger (†) after their name may not be combined together on the same weapon.

### Availability and Cost

Items available at the Trading Post are marked with their Availability Level (AL), such
as C (Common), R9 (Rarity 9), I13 (Illegal 13), or S6 (Scavenge 6).

#### Exclusive Items (E)

The availability of exclusive items is indicated with an 'E'. Please note that these exclusive items cannot be
purchased through the Trading Post and are only available within the gang's Equipment Lists.

#### Cost Adjustments: Diamond (♦)

If an item is available to a fighter through their equipment list at a different cost than
in the Trading Post, it will be marked accordingly. Fighters cannot purchase such items
from the Trading Post and must instead use their own equipment list.

Any differences in cost are shown for reference only, as House Equipment Lists no longer
exist – all equipment access and costs are now determined by individual fighter equipment lists.
Consult your Fighter's Equipment list during gang creation to cross-reference the item's cost.

#### Specific Trading Post Appendix: Caret (^)

If an item has been made available through a specific Trading Post Appendix, its origin will
be marked accordingly, even if it has later been added as a reference in other publications.
Discuss with your arbitrator if these are available in your campaign.
