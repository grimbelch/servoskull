# Underhells Campaign

[RAW ✔️](/docs/publication-index/rules-index) *The Book of Desolation, p61*

NecroRAW

Do not confuse this campaign with its 2-player version also
titled "[Underhells Campaign](/docs/campaigns/other-campaigns/underhells-campaign/)" published in *Necromunda: Hive Secundus Rulebook*.

> *"It's a long way from the Dust Wall to the Underhells,
> and every step of the way something's going to try to kill you…"*
> - Yola Xad, Port Grave Ferryman, Windsplinter Alliance

Hive Secundus is one of the most dangerous places on Necromunda. Even so, the lure of lost archeotech, ancient
data crystals and wealth beyond imagination draws treasure hunters from across the wastes. On the great
cartographs hung within the strategiums of the spires, the ruins of Hive Secundus are collared by the thick black
lines of the Dust Wall, an impenetrable edifice keeping the hellish denizens of the ruined city contained. In reality,
numerous ways through the wall exist, be they sneaking down forgotten tunnels, bribing corrupt garrison lords or
finding a section of the wall that has fallen into disrepair. It is through these secret ways that archeotech hunters
make their way into the forbidden zones beyond, and perhaps even all the way to the Secundus Abyss and the
broken city that rests in its depths…
