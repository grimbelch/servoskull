# Underhells Campaign (2-player version)

[RAW ✔️](/docs/publication-index/rules-index) *Necromunda: Hive Secundus Rulebook, p110*

NecroRAW

Do not confuse this campaign with its multi-player version also
titled "[Underhells Campaign](/docs/campaigns/underhells-campaign/)" published in *The Book of Desolation*.

> *"Nothing stirs up the nest like a group of greedy Tek-hunters
> poking around in the ruins and there is no better bait to draw out the beasts..."*  
> - Tamino Helmawr, 14th Trueborn Son of Lord Gerontius Helmawr

Hive Secundus is one of the most dangerous places
on Necromunda. Even so, the lure of lost archeotech,
ancient data crystals and wealth beyond imagination
draws treasure hunters from across the wastes.
Accompanying these daring fools are the Spyrers;
consummate killers equipped with the finest weapons
and armour the Imperial House can afford. While the
Tek-hunters, as these treasure seekers are known,
blunder through the gloom trying not to rouse the
locals, the Spyrer uses them as bait to draw out the
minions of the Malstrain Patriarch. For the Spyrer it is
a test of combat prowess against alien horrors without
end, while for the Tek-hunters, who know all too well
what they are to their temporary companion, it is the
only way to reach the bountiful depths of the ruins
without being torn to shreds.
