# Battlefield Set-up & Scenarios

[RAW ✔️](/docs/publication-index/rules-index) *Necromunda: Hive Secundus Rulebook, p120*

Fighting to recover data crystals or prove yourself
worthy of power within House Helmawr in the depths
of Hive Secundus is a non-stop struggle for survival.
Gangs battle through the claustrophobic warrens of the
Underhells, seeking to escape with treasures untold.

The section that follows presents rules for setting
up a battlefield to represent the Underhells of Hive
Secundus, along with seven scenarios for gangs
to fight out. These range from a desperate dash to
escape the closing net of a Malstrain ambush, to a
race to gather as many data crystals as possible before
being overrun!
