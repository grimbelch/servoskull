# 1. Into the Underhells

[RAW ✔️](/docs/publication-index/rules-index) *Necromunda: Hive Secundus Rulebook, p130*

> *"By the STC, these crystals are untouched!"*  
> - Vanlur Rakkstell, Tek-hunter, Prime Movers, House Van Saar

In this scenario, a gang scours the area for ancient data crystals.

## Battlefield

The Malstrain places both playmats with their long
edges touching and then sets up four doors on the
[Hunting Ground](/docs/campaigns/other-campaigns/underhells-campaign/battlefield-setup-scenarios/underhells-scenarios#the-hunting-ground).

## Crews

The Secundan Incursion gang uses the standard rules
for choosing a crew, as described on page 122, and
the Hybrid Selection (2+3) method. The Malstrain has
10 MP to build their forces with.

## Deployment

The Malstrain deploys six Brood Scum anywhere on the
battlefield. The Secundan Incursion gang then choose
a short battlefield edge and deploy their crew anywhere
within 1" of that battlefield edge.

## Data Crystals

This section of the Underhells is full to the brim with
hidden knowledge. The first time a Data Crystal is
successfully harvested from each Data Crystal Stack,
the fighter performing the action gains an additional
Data Crystal.

## Reinforcements

At the end of each End phase, D3 Reinforcements
arrive for the Malstrain.

## Ending the Battle

The battle ends after eight rounds or if at the start of
any round, the Secundan Incursion have no models
remaining on the battlefield.

## Victory

If the Secundan Incursion successfully harvest each
Data Crystal Stack at least once, they are victorious.
Any other result is a victory for the Malstrain.

## Rewards

#### Experience

Each fighter that successfully harvests at least one
Data Crystal Stack earns an additional 1 XP.
