# 2. Brood Ambush

[RAW ✔️](/docs/publication-index/rules-index) *Necromunda: Hive Secundus Rulebook, p131*

> *"They're all around us!"*  
> - Laateth Tekoth, Daeyglow Dragons, House Van Saar

In this scenario, a gang must survive a sudden ambush by the Malstrain.

## Battlefield

The Malstrain places both playmats with their short
edges touching and then sets up five doors on the
[Hunting Ground](/docs/campaigns/other-campaigns/underhells-campaign/battlefield-setup-scenarios/underhells-scenarios#the-hunting-ground).

## Crews

The Secundan Incursion gang uses the standard rules
for choosing a crew, as described on page 122, and
the Random Selection (D3+3) method. The Malstrain
has 12 MP to build their forces with.

## Deployment

The Secundan Incursion gang deploy their starting
crew within 6" of the centre of the battlefield. The
Malstrain then deploys five Brood Scum within 1" of
the short battlefield edges.

## Ambush

The Secundan Incursion gang (except for the Spyrer)
may not flee the battlefield in the usual way. Instead, if
a Secundan Incursion gang fighter is within 1" of one
of the short battlefield edges at the start of the End
phase, they successfully escape the ambush and are
removed from the battlefield.

## Reinforcements

At the end of each End phase, two Reinforcements
arrive for the Malstrain.

## Ending the Battle

The battle ends after six rounds or if at the start of any
round, the Secundan Incursion gang have no models
remaining on the battlefield.

## Victory

If at least two Secundan Incursion fighters successfully
escape the ambush, they are victorious. Any other
result is a victory for the Malstrain.

## Rewards

#### Experience

If a fighter successfully escapes the ambush, they earn
an additional 1 XP.
