# 3. Data Crystal Harvest

[RAW ✔️](/docs/publication-index/rules-index) *Necromunda: Hive Secundus Rulebook, p132*

> *"Behold the libraries of Secundus in all their fallen glory!"*  
> - Gorbith Crankfoot, Port Mallius Ferryman

In this scenario, a gang must grab as many data crystals as
they can before the Malstrain forces hunt them down!

## Battlefield

The Malstrain places both playmats with their long
edges touching and then sets up four doors on the
[Hunting Ground](/docs/campaigns/other-campaigns/underhells-campaign/battlefield-setup-scenarios/underhells-scenarios#the-hunting-ground).

## Crews

The Secundan Incursion gang uses the standard rules
for choosing a crew, as described on page 122, and
the Hybrid Selection (3+D3) method. The Malstrain has
11 MP to build their forces with.

## Deployment

The Malstrain deploys six Brood Scum anywhere on
the battlefield. The Secundan Incursion gang then
choose a short battlefield edge and deploys their crew
anywhere within 1" of that battlefield edge.

## Ancient Crystal Library

The ancient library's storage banks are easily
overloaded. If a Data Crystal Stack is harvested
two times or more in a single round then it
automatically shuts down. It cannot be harvested in
the following round, but can be harvested as usual in
subsequent rounds.

## Reinforcements

At the end of each End phase, D3+1 Reinforcements
arrive for the Malstrain.

## Ending the Battle

The battle ends after six rounds or if at the start of any
round, the Secundan Incursion gang have no models
remaining on the battlefield.

## Victory

If the Secundan Incursion gang successfully harvests
at least six Data Crystals, they are victorious. Any other
result is a victory for the Malstrain.

## Rewards

#### Experience

If any fighter harvests at least three Data Crystals, that
fighter earns an additional 1 XP.
