# 5. Champion of the Malstrain

[RAW ✔️](/docs/publication-index/rules-index) *Necromunda: Hive Secundus Rulebook, p134*

> *"Come meet my mossst favoured ssson..."*  
> - Helogoth, Hybrid Primus, Cult of the Second Son

In this scenario, a gang must slay a champion of the Malstrain.

## Battlefield

The Malstrain places both playmats with their long
edges touching and then sets up four doors on the
[Hunting Ground](/docs/campaigns/other-campaigns/underhells-campaign/battlefield-setup-scenarios/underhells-scenarios#the-hunting-ground).

## Crews

The Secundan Incursion gang uses the standard rules
for choosing a crew, as described on page 122, and
the Hybrid Selection (3+D3) method. The Malstrain has
12 MP to build their forces with.

## Deployment

The Malstrain deploys the Malstrain Champion
(see below) in the centre of the battlefield and then
deploys four Brood Scum anywhere on the battlefield.
The Secundan Incursion gang then choose a short
It battlefield edge and deploys their crew anywhere within
i 1" of that battlefield edge.

## Malstrain Champion

The Malstrain includes one additional Malstrain
Genestealer in their force, which does not cost any
MP. This Malstrain Genestealer is a champion of the
Malstrain - increase their Strength, Toughness and
Attacks by one.

## Reinforcements

At the end of each End phase, 1 Reinforcement arrives
for the Malstrain.

## Ending the Battle

The battle ends after eight rounds or if at the start
of any round, the Secundan Incursion gang have no
models remaining on the battlefield.

## Victory

If the Malstrain Champion has been removed from the
battlefield, the Secundan Incursion gang are victorious.
Any other result is a victory for the Malstrain.

## Rewards

#### Experience

If a fighter takes the Malstrain Champion Out of Action,
they earn an additional D3 XP.
