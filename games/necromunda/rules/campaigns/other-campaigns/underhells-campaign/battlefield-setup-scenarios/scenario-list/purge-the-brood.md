# 6. Purge the Brood

[RAW ✔️](/docs/publication-index/rules-index) *Necromunda: Hive Secundus Rulebook, p135*

> *"Kill them! Kill them all!"*  
> - Kaerl Jergaan, Tek Warriors, House Van Saar

In this scenario, a gang must clear an area of the Malstrain Patriarch's influence.

## Battlefield

The Malstrain places both playmats with their long
edges touching and then sets up five doors on the
[Hunting Ground](/docs/campaigns/other-campaigns/underhells-campaign/battlefield-setup-scenarios/underhells-scenarios#the-hunting-ground).

## Crews

The Secundan Incursion gang uses the standard rules
for choosing a crew, as described on page 122, and
the Custom Selection (5) method. The Malstrain has
12 MP to build their forces with.

## Deployment

The Malstrain deploys five Brood Scum anywhere on
the battlefield. The Secundan Incursion gang then
choose a long battlefield edge and deploys their crew
anywhere within 1" of that battlefield edge.

## Mindless Hordes

When Malstrain Reinforcements arrive, they are placed
by the Secundan Incursion gang on a roll of 13
instead of the usual 12.

## Reinforcements

At the end of each End phase, D3+1 Reinforcements
arrive for the Malstrain.

## Ending the Battle

The battle ends after six rounds or if at the start of any
round, the Secundan Incursion gang have no models
remaining on the battlefield.

## Victory

If the Spyrer has added at least six to their Kill Count
during the battle and the Secundan Incursion gang
successfully harvest at least three Data Crystals,
they are victorious. Any other result is a victory for
the Malstrain.

## Rewards

#### Experience

If the Secundan Incursion gang win then the Spyrer
earns an additional D3 XP.
