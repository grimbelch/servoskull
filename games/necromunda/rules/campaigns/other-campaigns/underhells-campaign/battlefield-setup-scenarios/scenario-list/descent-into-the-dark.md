# 4. Descent Into the Dark

[RAW ✔️](/docs/publication-index/rules-index) *Necromunda: Hive Secundus Rulebook, p133*

> *"That's an awfully long drop..."*  
> - Tarn Fornholm, Hellrunners, House Van Saar

In this scenario, a gang must attempt to cross a dangerous region taking them deeper into the Underhells.

## Battlefield

The Malstrain places both playmats with their long
edges touching and then sets up six doors on the
[Hunting Ground](/docs/campaigns/other-campaigns/underhells-campaign/battlefield-setup-scenarios/underhells-scenarios#the-hunting-ground).

## Crews

The Secundan Incursion gang uses the standard rules
for choosing a crew, as described on page 122, and
the Custom Selection (7) method. The Malstrain has
11 MP to build their forces with.

## Deployment

The Malstrain deploys seven Brood Scum anywhere
on the battlefield. The Secundan Incursion gang then
chooses a long battlefield edge and deploys their crew
anywhere within 1" of that battlefield edge.

## Deeper Into The Underhells

Any Secundan Incursion fighter may perform the Delve
Deeper (Basic) action if they are within 1" of the long
battlefield edge opposite their deployment zone:

**Delve Deeper (Basic):** The fighter ventures further
into the depths of the Underhells - remove them from
the battlefield.

## Reinforcements

At the end of each End phase, 3 Reinforcements arrive
for the Malstrain.

## Ending the Battle

The battle ends after eight rounds or if at the start
of any round, the Secundan Incursion gang have no
models remaining on the battlefield.

## Victory

If the Secundan Incursion gang successfully harvest
at least three Data Crystals and have at least three
fighters Delve Deeper, they are victorious. Any other
result is a victory for the Malstrain.

## Rewards

#### Experience

Any fighter that manages to Delve Deeper earns an
additional 1 XP.
