# Using The Campaign

[RAW ✔️](/docs/publication-index/rules-index) *Necromunda: Hive Secundus Rulebook, p111*

The Necromunda: Underhells Campaign is a complete
narrative campaign designed for two players. It follows
groups of daring Secundan Incursion gangs delving
into the ancient ruins of Secundus in search of fabled
data crystals. One player takes on the role of these
explorers and works against the Malstrain of the
Underhells, controlled by the other player, by playing
through a series of scenarios, and hopefully escaping
with their lives!

## The Arbitrator

The Underhells Campaign requires one of the players
to take on the role of an Arbitrator. It is the Arbitrator's
job to keep track of victories, offer guidance for the
players and control any random events or elements.
In the Underhells Campaign, the Arbitrator's
responsibilities include:

- The Arbitrator takes on the role of the Malstrain,
  opposing the other player as they hunt for
  archeo-treasures.
- Helping the other player to create their Secundan
  Expeditionary gang.
- Keeping track of the Secundan Incursion gangs'
  progress through the Underhells and keeping
  their player informed as to the progression of
  the campaign.
- Deciding when each phase of the campaign begins
  and ends, as well as when the overall campaign
  begins and ends.

## Secundan Incursion Gangs And The Malstrain

There are two opposing forces in an Underhells
Campaign: the Secundan Incursion gangs and the
Malstrain. Secundan Incursion gangs are covered on
page 139.

Secundan Incursion gangs created for an Underhells
Campaign have an initial budget of 1,250 credits
instead of the usual 1,000 credits.

The Malstrain controls the twisted horrors of the
Underhells in the form of Brood Scum, Malstrain
Tyramites and Malstrain Genestealers. Unlike the
Secundan Incursion gangs, the forces of the Malstrain
are effectively limitless; each scenario will grant the
Malstrain player a number of Malstrain Points (MP) to
build their forces from (see page 148).

## Controlling Territories

The focus of the Underhells Campaign is the
exploration of the Underhells of Hive Secundus and,
hopefully for the Secundan Incursion gangs, escaping
with as many data crystals as possible. To this end,
each campaign cycle a battle will be fought for control
of one of the locations of the Underhells, representing
the route the Secundan Incursion gangs take in their
exploration of Hive Secundus.

If they manage to gain control of them, each Territory
will grant the Secundan Incursion gang a benefit,
called a Boon.

## Pre- and Post-battle Sequence

The Underhells Campaign uses all of the standard
rules for the pre- and post-battle sequences presented
earlier in this book, including the gaining and spending
of XP, with the following exceptions:

### Pre-battle Sequence

In an Underhells Campaign, the Recruit Hired Guns
and Gang Tactics steps of the pre-battle
sequence are not used.

### Post-battle Sequence

In an Underhells Campaign, the Collect Income
and Visit the Trading Post steps are not used. In
addition, only the following post-battle
actions may be performed:

- **Medical Treatment:** The fighter performs a Medical
  Escort action (see page 96). This action does not
  cost any credits.
- **Fabricate Weapon:** The fighter makes an
  Intelligence test; if the test is passed then a fighter
  in their gang may be equipped with a weapon from
  their Equipment List that costs no more than 25
  credits for free.
- **Fabricate Wargear:** The fighter makes an
  Intelligence test; if the test is passed then a fighter in
  their gang may be equipped with an item of Wargear
  from their Equipment List that costs no more than
  25 credits for free.
- **Find Hidden Tech:** The fighter may exchange data
  crystals for Archeo-loot as described on page 115.

## Winning

Victory in the Underhells Campaign is determined by
whichever side manages to win the battle to control the
Broodthrone at the end of the campaign.
