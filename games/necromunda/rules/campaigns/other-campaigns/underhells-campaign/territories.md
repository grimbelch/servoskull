# Territories

[RAW ✔️](/docs/publication-index/rules-index) *Necromunda: Hive Secundus Rulebook, p116*

## Level One

### Rogue Doc Shop

*Once this was the home of a rogue doc,
where gangs would go to get patched up. Now
abandoned, there may still be something of
use here.*

**Battle Effect:** The Malstrain has +1 MP available
to create their forces.

**Secundan Incursion Gang Boon:** While the
Secundan Incursion gang controls this territory,
a fighter of their choice starts each battle with a
Nano-med Archeo-loot card that is discarded in
the Wrap-up step of the post-battle sequence if
not used.

---

### Bullet Den

*Despite the destruction wrought upon the hive,
some areas still remain untouched. This bullet den
still has supplies of ammunition left, which makes
it an essential stop for those whose supplies are
running low.*

**Battle Effect:** The Malstrain has no modifier on
the MP they have available to create their forces.

**Secundan Incursion Gang Boon:** While the
Secundan Incursion gang controls this territory,
they may re-roll a single failed Ammo test
each round.

---

### Drinking Hole

Before the hive's destruction, this was a drinking
den such as could be found in any hive. Now it is
abandoned, the Cult of the Second Son having no
need for recreation.

**Battle Effect:** The Malstrain has +1 MP available
to create their forces.

**Secundan Incursion Gang Boon:** While the
Secundan Incursion gang controls this territory,
they automatically pass the first Cool test they
are called upon to make each battle (no dice roll
is necessary).

---

### Old Agri Dome

*This area was once a lush garden of fruit and rare
plants - now it is an overgrown and rotting hell,
its floor a tangle of desiccated roots and leaf litter,
creating fertile soil for more esoteric plants.*

**Battle Effect:** The Malstrain has no modifier on
the MP they have available to create their forces.

**Secundan Incursion Gang Boon:** While the
Secundan Incursion gang controls this territory,
they may clear one In Recovery box on their gang
roster at the end of the post-battle sequence
each battle.

---

### Abandoned Tek-hunter Camp

*Some time ago, this area was used as a camp by
another expedition and some of their gear can still
be found here.*

**Battle Effect:** The Malstrain has +1 MP available
to create their forces.

**Secundan Incursion Gang Boon:** While the
Secundan Incursion gang controls this territory,
once per battle they may re-roll a single failed
armour save.

---

## Descent One

### Dark Chambers

*A series of chambers descend into the Underhells,
each more perilous than the last.*

**Battle Effect:** The Malstrain has +3 MP available
to create their forces.

**Secundan Incursion Gang Boon:** While the
Secundan Incursion gang controls this territory,
they earn an additional 1 XP for taking Malstrain
Genestealers Out of Action.

---

### Ancient Conveyor

*The only way onwards is to descend upon the
hive's ancient conveyor.*

**Battle Effect:** The Malstrain has +2 MP available
to create their forces.

**Secundan Incursion Gang Boon:** While the
Secundan Incursion gang controls this territory,
they may re-roll the result when determining which
scenario will be played.

---

### Nano-protein Vats

*The hunters must climb through vats of ancient
nano-protein to reach the lower levels.*

**Battle Effect:** The Malstrain has +4 MP available
to create their forces.

**Secundan Incursion Gang Boon:** While the
Secundan Incursion gang controls this territory,
they permanently negate the effects of a single
Lasting Injury. Note this may only be used once
per campaign.

---

## Level Two

### Lair Of The Mind Worm

*The hunters enter the lair of a powerful agent of
the Patriarch. If defeated, it may be possible to use
it to disrupt its plans.*

**Battle Effect:** The Malstrain has +3 MP to create
their forces.

**Secundan Incursion Gang Boon:** While the
Secundan Incursion gang controls this Territory,
they may declare they are using this Boon at the
start of any End phase. For the duration of that
round, they place the Malstrain's Reinforcements
on a roll of 14 instead of 12. This Boon may only
be used once per battle.

---

### Crystal Vaults

*The glory of fallen Secundus can still be seen in
these mirrored glittering domes and their piles of
unclaimed treasures.*

**Battle Effect:** The Malstrain has +4 MP available
to create their forces.

**Secundan Incursion Gang Boon:** While the
Secundan Incursion gang controls this territory,
they treat the Harvest Data Crystals (Basic) action
as a Simple action.

---

### Old Factorum

*Disused and rusting machines clutter this level,
their pistons and gears sometimes springing to life
in a parody of their previous glory.*

**Battle Effect:** The Malstrain has +3 MP available
to create their forces.

**Secundan Incursion Gang Boon:** While the
Secundan Incursion gang controls this territory,
a single fighter may increase their armour save by
1 (to a maximum of 2+). This benefit cannot be
applied to the Spyrer.

---

### Gallery Of Sorrow

*The walls of these chambers are covered in
murals of the grandeur of Secundus - people
engaged in industry or inspired innovation.*

**Battle Effect:** The Malstrain has +2 MP available
to create their forces.

**Secundan Incursion Gang Boon:** While
the Secundan Incursion gang controls this
territory, D3 fighters of their choice gain 1
additional XP in the Receive Rewards step of the
post-battle sequence.

---

### Abandoned Prison

*Even Secundus had criminal elements, and it was
in places like these they were sealed away from
the rest of hive society.*

**Battle Effect:** The Malstrain has +3 MP available
to create their forces.

**Secundan Incursion Gang Boon:** While the
Secundan Incursion gang controls this territory,
once per battle they may open or close a single
door on the battlefield before rolling for Priority.

---

## Descent Two

### Fighting Platforms

*The hunters must battle their way down a series
of ancient arena fighting platforms that hang over
the abyss.*

**Battle Effect:** The Malstrain has +5 MP available
to create their forces.

**Secundan Incursion Gang Boon:** While the
Secundan Incursion gang controls this territory,
a fighter of their choice gains a random skill from
one of their Primary Skill Sets.

---

### Hunters Hunted

*As they delve deeper into the Underhells, another
hunter stalks the darkness.*

**Battle Effect:** The Malstrain has +6 MP available
to create their forces.

**Secundan Incursion Gang Boon:** While the
Secundan Incursion gang controls this territory,
they may include a second Spyrer in their gang
for a single battle. This fighter is included in the
starting crew in addition to any allowed by the
scenario and may be equipped however the
Secundan Expeditionary player wishes.

---

### Old Battle Site

*Long ago a furious battle between hivers and
xenos was fought here.*

**Battle Effect:** The Malstrain has +4 MP available
to create their forces.

**Secundan Incursion Gang Boon:** While the
Secundan Incursion gang controls this territory,
they may perform the Fabricate Weapon and
Fabricate Wargear post-battle action an additional
time each for free.

---

## Level Three

### Auto Turret Defence Network

*A relic of the war, this chamber has remained
preserved behind security fields and auto-defences,
both of which the explorers can now
turn against the Patriarch's minions.*

Battle Effect: The Malstrain has +4 MP available
to create their forces.
Secundan Incursion Gang Boon: While the
Secundan Incursion gang controls this territory,
any Malstrain Genestealers deployed by the
Malstrain enter play with a Flesh Wound.

---

### The Pit

*A place of magnificent treasures and
unfathomable dangers.*

**Battle Effect:** The Malstrain has +5 MP available
to create their forces.

**Secundan Incursion Gang Boon:** While
the Secundan Incursion gang controls this
territory, they add 3 Data Crystals to their
Stash during the Receive Rewards step of each
post-battle sequence.

---

### Toxic Cistern

*The filth of the hive flowed through this chamber
and its floor is still covered in stagnant slime.*

**Battle Effect:** The Malstrain has +7 MP available
to create their forces.

**Secundan Incursion Gang Boon:** While the
Secundan Incursion gang controls this territory, D3
weapons with the Melee trait gain the Toxin trait.

---

### Endless Chasm

*A rift leading down into the bowels of the planet
created when the thermal vent cracked.*

**Battle Effect:** The Malstrain has +7 MP available
to create their forces.

**Secundan Incursion Gang Boon:** While the
Secundan Incursion gang controls this territory, a
fighter of their choice gains the Infiltrate skill.

---

### Brood Maze

*The coiling tunnels seem alive with the madness
of the Malstrain Patriarch.*

**Battle Effect:** The Malstrain has +5 MP available
to create their forces.

**Secundan Incursion Gang Boon:** While the
Secundan Incursion gang controls this territory,
they may increase the number of fighters in their
starting crew by 1. This additional fighter is always
chosen by the Secundan Incursion gang.

---

### Broodthrone

*Deep in the heart of Secundus sits the master of
the Cult of the Second Son, their minions crowded
around them.*

**Battle Effect:** The Malstrain has +14 MP available
to create their forces. The scenario played for
control of this Territory must be Champion of
the Malstrain.

**Final Encounter:** This is the final climatic
encounter for the Secundan Incursion gang.
Whoever wins this Territory wins the campaign!
