# Badzone Enforcers In Dominion Campaigns

[RAW ✔️](/docs/publication-index/rules-index) *Bastions of Law, p95*

Completely cut off as they are from any hope of
resupply, Badzone Enforcer patrols are forced to
claim and work territories in the underhive in order
to maintain their nominal control of the local area.

In a Dominion Campaign, Badzone Enforcer
patrols are treated just like any other gang in
most respects; instead of beginning the campaign
with a Settlement Territory, they start with a
Precinct-Fortress Territory that they cannot lose,
representing their fortified headquarters. They
can take control of unoccupied Territories during
the Occupation phase and can seize control of
Territories from other gangs during the Takeover
phase. They claim Territory Boons just like any
other gang, and gain enhanced Boons for a Narco
Den Territory as if they were an Escher gang and
from a Gambling Den Territory as if they were a
Delaque gang.

## Unique Territory – Palanite Precinct

*Isolated from their fellow enforcers and forced
to become completely self-sufficient, Badzone
Enforcers turn their precincts into true fortresses,
impervious to all attack and the base for all of
their operations (both legal and illegal).*

### Territory Boon

This Territory grants the following Boons:

**Income:** The gang earns D6x10 credits from this
Territory when collecting income.

**Reputation:** While they control this Territory,
the gang gains +1 Reputation.

**Special:** If this gang took at least four enemy
fighters Out of Action this cycle, they earn an
additional 2D6x1O credits from this Territory
when collecting income as they cow the local
populace into paying greater protection money.
