# Narrative Scenarios

[RAW ✔️](/docs/publication-index/rules-index) *The Aranthian Succession – Spire of Primus, p81*

## Narrative Scenarios

*"It's not that I love House Helmawr, but I'm obviously
working to put them back. Better the enemy you know
than an all-consuming psychic compulsion bleeding
into your brain."*

Silberlant Sevos, the Infotek

In this section, you will find a selection of eight
narrative scenarios themed around the events of Lady
Haera's final fight against the usurper Ozostium. Each
of these can either be played as part of a campaign,
or as a Skirmish battle.

There are also guidance notes included if you wish to
play them as 'historical' scenarios to fight out the
gruelling reconquest of the spire by Lady Haera and
her allies, culminating in the final fight for
Necromunda.
