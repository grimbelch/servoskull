# Part 4 - Reconquest of Primum

[RAW ✔️](/docs/publication-index/rules-index) *The Aranthian Succession – Spire of Primus, p51*

Following on from the events of *Necromunda:
The Aranthian Succession - Ruins of Jardlan* and
*Necromunda: The Book of Desolation*, Ozostium
has claimed rulership of Necromunda. However,
Lady Haera Helmawr has allied with her brother,
Tamino to recover a weapon capable of ending
even an undying Aranthian: a Sthenian-pattern
Spyder Suit.

Now is the final stand for Lady Haera, her Spyrers and
those gangs loyal to the Imperium. It is up to these
holdout forces to strike deep into Hive Primus and
reclaim the spire in the name of Lord Gerontius
Helmawr. Yet it is not only Ozostium's loyal Enforcers
and gangs they must contend with - at the very
peak of the hive, deep within the Imperial Spire,
the Aranthian himself waits for one final showdown...
