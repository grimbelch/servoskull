# Using The Campaign

[RAW ✔️](/docs/publication-index/rules-index) *The Aranthian Succession – Spire of Primus, p52*

The Reconquest of Primus Campaign is a complete
narrative campaign that can be used alongside the
Necromunda core rules. It incorporates many of
the existing Necromunda campaign rules, adding
additional special rules to recreate Haera's quest
to reclaim her father's seat of power. The campaign
is divided into two phases, separated by a period of
Downtime: the Ascent phase and the Summit phase.

In the Ascent phase, gangs fight to seize or secure
Spire Districts. Those supporting the Imperial House
will be looking to take back chunks of the spire from
those supporting Ozostium, whilst those defending
Necromunda's new ruler must hinder their attackers
as much as possible before they reach the top.

Then, in the Summit phase, Lady Haera and her
gangs reach the uppermost towers of the spire where
Lord Helmawr's palace, the Imperial Spire, is located.
Here they must prepare for one final showdown with
Ozostium and his allies.

This campaign allows players to use their gangs
from the third part of the Succession Campaign in
*Necromunda: The Aranthian Succession - Ruins of
Jardlan*, allowing them to continue to expand and grow
their power. This book covers the final phase of fighting
and the ultimate struggle for victory between the
Imperial and Aranthian sides.

## The Arbitrator

The Reconquest of Primus Campaign requires one
of the players to take on the role of an Arbitrator.
It is the Arbitrator's job to keep track of victories,
offer guidance to the players and control any random
events or elements. In the Reconquest of Primus
Campaign, the Arbitrator's responsibilities include:

- Organising the players and making a list of all of the
  gangs involved in the campaign.
- Keeping the players informed as to the progression
  of the campaign and keeping track of the control of
  Spire Districts.
- Deciding when each phase of the campaign begins
  and ends, as well as when the overall campaign
  begins and ends.

## The Gangs

Every faction on Necromunda has an interest
in who wins the fight for control over the world.
The Reconquest of Primus Campaign can be
played with any Necromunda gang. Also, during such
a period of unrest, each House was split in its loyalties
meaning that intra-House conflict was common.

## Controlling Spire Districts

Central to the Reconquest of Primus Campaign is the
control of Spire Districts. At the start of the campaign,
gangs siding with the Imperial House will be attempting
to climb the spire and retake as many Spire Districts
as possible to consolidate power and gain resources.
Gangs supporting House Aranthus will need to stop
Lady Haera's supporters and reinforce important
sectors of the spire with their presence before they
can fall to the enemy. These are represented by a
special kind of Territory known as Spire Districts.
As Lady Haera and her allies draw closer to Ozostium,
it is important to gain control of these locations to
either impede or aid the reconquest. Players will start
with a few Spire Districts under their control, and must
try to capture more.

## Pre- and Post-battle Sequence

The Reconquest of Primus Campaign uses all of the
standard rules for pre- and post-battle sequences
presented in the *Necromunda Core Rulebook*,
including the gaining and spending of XP.

## Winning

Victory in the Reconquest of Primus Campaign is
more than just controlling the Spire Districts. Only the
last leader standing can truly call themselves ruler of
Necromunda. However, there are other triumphs to
be earned for champions of individual glory, a full list
of which can be found on page 63.
