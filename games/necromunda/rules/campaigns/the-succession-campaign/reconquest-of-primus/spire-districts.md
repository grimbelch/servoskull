# Spire Districts

[RAW ✔️](/docs/publication-index/rules-index) *The Aranthian Succession – Spire of Primus, p70*

During a Reconquest of Primus Campaign, gangs fight
for control of a special kind of Territory called Spire
Districts. These are locations within Hive Primus' spire
that those allied to the Imperial House are fighting
through to reach Ozostium.

Each Spire District has Battle Effects that are applied
to any battle fought where that Spire District is the
stake. Additionally, they grant the holder a Boon,
which is collected during Step 4: Collect Income of
the post-battle sequence.

Each gang also controls a Clan Embassy Spire District
that can never be challenged for as the stake for a
battle and they can never lose control of.

## Spire District Boons

Each Spire District grants the gang that controls it a
benefit called a Boon. This Boon might be income,
an additional gang member, a piece of rare or unique
equipment or a special rule.

A gang receives the benefits of a Spire District's Boon
so long as it holds that Spire District. Once the Spire
District is lost to another gang, the Boon is lost.

### Income

If a Spire District provides income for the gang that
controls it, the gang adds the amount of credits shown
to their Stash when generating income after all battles,
including the battle in which it was won.

### Recruit

Some Spire Districts grant gangs new fighters,
Hired Guns or Hangers-on as recruits to the gang.
These recruits incur no cost to recruit but do add to
the Gang Rating and Wealth value.

Any gang fighter recruited from the gang's House List
has no cost to recruit, but any equipment that is not
part of the fighter's basic cost must be purchased at
the normal cost, or supplied from the gang's Stash.

Any Hired Gun recruited as a Boon has no cost,
and will remain with the gang for as long as they
control the Spire District that grants the Boon.

Any Hanger-on recruited as a Boon does not count
towards the maximum number of Hangers-on the gang
may normally have, may be recruited without meeting
the normal Reputation requirement and remains with
the gang for as long as they control the Spire District
that grants the boon.

### Equipment

The gang adds the listed equipment type to its Stash.
It is gained in the Post-battle Actions step of the
post-battle sequence, immediately after the battle
in which the Spire District was won and can be
distributed amongst eligible fighters during the next
step of the same post-battle sequence.

The equipment can never be sold or traded and if the
Spire District is ever lost, then so is the equipment as
it can no longer be maintained without the expertise
present within the Spire District.

### Special

The Boon grants a unique benefit to the gang that
holds the Spire District. The benefit follows the rules
listed in the relevant entry for the Spire District. If the
Spire District is ever lost then so is the benefit.

### Enhanced Boons

Some Spire Districts are more important to gangs
from a particular House - an Escher gang might
be able to grudgingly gain credits from the Grand
Cathedral Imperialis, but to a Cawdor gang that
same sacred site is a beacon of faith.

Many Spire Districts grant gangs of a named House
one or more Enhanced Boons, listed below the
Spire District Boons for each of the Spire Districts
detailed on the following pages. If the Enhanced
Boon is of the same type as the standard Spire
District Boon (Income, Recruit, Equipment,
Reputation or Special) then it replaces the
standard Spire District Boon.

The remaining standard Spire District Boons listed
for a Spire District are gained as normal by the gang
controlling that Spire District, regardless of any
Enhanced Boons.

### Gaining and Controlling Spire Districts

The gaining of Spire Districts is at the heart of a
Reconquest of Primus Campaign. Spire Districts are
gained by winning a battle that has a Spire District
as the stake. The only other way of gaining a Spire
District is if two players agree to trade one in
exchange for a Captive.

## Reconquest Of Primus Campaign Spire Districts

---

### CLAN EMBASSY

Every Clan House has an embassy from where they once communicated with their patrons in the spire. Now they
have been transformed into strongholds from which their gangs can recruit reinforcements and strike out at other
Spire Districts.

#### SPIRE DISTRICT BOON

**Income:** The gang earns 2D6x1O credits from this Spire District when collecting income.

**Recruit:** The gang may choose to roll two D6 after every battle. On a roll of 6 on either dice, the gang may recruit
a single Juve from their House List for free. If both dice come up as 6, then the gang may recruit a Ganger from
their House List for free.

---

### Rejuve Centre

**Ace of Diamonds**

Long are the lives of nobles that can afford it.
With expensive technology imported from across
the stars, the Rejuve Centre is not quite a
fountain of eternal youth, but it will certainly
give you a couple of extra centuries with enough
stimm-cell injections, plumping bacteria and
neural refreshers.

**Battle Effects:** In a battle where this Spire District
is the stake, during the Recovery & Restart step
of the End phase, Seriously Injured fighters are all
considered to have rolled a Flesh Wound, there is
no need to roll any Injury dice.

#### Spire District Boon

**Income:** The gang earns 2D6x10 credits from this
Spire District when collecting income.

**Recruit:** Instead of collecting income, a gang can
choose to recruit a Rogue Doc for free.

---

### Spyrer Factoria

**Two of Diamonds**

Though the most monstrous patterns of Spyder rig
are thought to be lost, those gleaming green shells
turned out by Gerontius Helmawr's private factoria
are still fearsome weapons in their own right.
When a Helmawr comes of age, they are escorted
to pristine white warehouses to be fitted for a
custom set of armour - and have whichever limbs
that don't fit inside it removed.

**Battle Effects:** In a battle where this Spire District
is the stake, Spyrers can re-roll one or both dice
when rolling for Hunting Rig Glitches.
All gangs ignore the Out of Ammo symbol on the
Firepower dice.

#### Spire District Boon

**Special:** The gang earns 3D6x10 credits from this
Spire District when collecting income.

---

### The Silvered Pavilion

**Three of Diamonds**

In the heart of the spire lies the Silvered Pavilion,
a festival ground surrounded by several palaces
belonging to the noble houses. The artificial
screens lining the dome ceiling are filled with
stars every few cycles as denizens of the spire
take part in drinking competitions, provocative
dances and the occasional duel to the death.

**Battle Effects:** In a battle where this Spire District
is the stake, failed Leadership tests may
be re-rolled.

#### Spire District Boon

**Income:** The gang earns 3D6x10 credits from this
Spire District when collecting income.

---

### Limb Farms

**Four of Diamonds**

When a Spyre tires of the thrill of slaughter and
has earned the right to return to high society,
it is not easy to return their distended, often
dismembered bodies to their original state.
Luckily for young debutantes, the Limb Farms are
on hand to cultivate new body parts from their
cell samples to avoid ugly and unfashionable
cybernetic replacements.

**Battle Effects:** In a battle where this Spire District
is the stake, roll a D6 whenever a fighter suffers
a Flesh Wound. On a 5+, they do not suffer the
Flesh Wound.

#### Spire District Boon

**Income:** The gang earns D6x10 credits from this
Spire District when collecting income.

**Special:** Instead of collecting income, a gang may
remove a Lasting Injury from a fighter. That fighter
then goes into Recovery.

---

### Psykanarium

**Five of Diamonds**

This Psykanarium is one of many, each kept
carefully secret by Lord Helmawr. Its dome of
blackstone-laced titanium cells are constantly
filled with echoed screams as the psykers within
are conditioned to serve him.

**Battle Effects:** In a battle where this Spire District
is the stake, Willpower tests may be re-rolled when
manifesting Wyrd Powers.

#### Spire District Boon

**Income:** The gang earns 2D6x10 credits from this
Spire District when collecting income.

**Recruit:** Instead of collecting income, a gang can
choose to recruit an Unsanctioned Indentured
Psyker (see page 102) to their gang for free.

---

### The Imperial Hangar

**Six of Diamonds**

Being powerful and popular comes with myriad
threats, and sometimes a quick escape is
necessary. This private landing bay is nestled
below the entrance to the Imperial Spire and
includes a range of expensive speeders in every
shape and colour.

**Battle Effects:** In a battle where this Spire District
is the stake, gangs can re-roll one Injury dice when
making Recovery tests.

#### Spire District Boon

**Income:** The gang earns 2D6x10 credits from this
Spire District when collecting income.

---

### Ulanti Grand Banquet Hall

**Seven of Diamonds**

There is something to be said for stuffing yourself
with food until bursting and then purging your
body so you can experience a completely different
palate of flavours. The Ullanti have just the thing
in their banquet hall, rows of lounging beds are
interspersed with tables, each with an ornate
bucket tucked beneath for the expulsion of
bodily fluids.

**Battle Effects:** In a battle where this Spire District
is the stake, Standing and Active fighters may
perform Consume Delicacies (Double) action.

**Consume Delicacies (Double):** The fighter may
remove all Flesh Wounds and recover all Wounds
they have lost in this battle. However, for the
remainder of the battle they will automatically fail
any Willpower tests they make. Each fighter may
only perform this action once per battle.

In addition, at the end of battle, any Seriously
Injured fighters are automatically considered to be
Out Cold.

#### Spire District Boon

**Income:** The gang earns 2D6x10 credits from this
Spire District when collecting income.

**Recruit:** Instead of collecting income, a gang can
choose to recruit a Slopper for free.

---

### Jagerkin Forest Domes

**Eight of Diamonds**

The domes below the palace of House Greim
are an artificial wonder of synthetic forests. It is
here that their Jagerkin train and hunt for sport,
though it is also quite a convenient place to 'lose'
political enemies whilst on a House tour.

**Battle Effects:** In a battle where this Spire District
is the stake, the Visibility (10") rule is in effect.

#### Spire District Boon

**Income:** The gang earns 2D6x10 credits from this
Spire District when collecting income.

**Recruit:** Instead of collecting income, a gang can
choose to recruit a Hive Watcher (see page 104)
for free.

---

### Haus De Catallus

**Nine of Diamonds**

The spire's prime opera venue is the pride of
House Catallus. This theatre is carved from solid
gold, each seat lined with purple velvet. Its prop
rooms contain real and extremely rare weapons,
after all, a romantic drama is not complete without
a little bloodletting between rival suitors.

**Battle Effects:** In a battle where this Spire District
is the stake, no scenery should be placed within
6" of the centre of the battlefield. This area is
considered to be the Stage. Any melee attacks
made while within the Stage may re-roll failed rolls
to hit and to wound.

#### Spire District Boon

**Special:** Any melee weapon the gang purchases
in each post-battle sequence has its Availability
level decreased by 1.

---

### Onmyodo Temple

**Ten of Diamonds**

The training halls of the Omnyodo Covens are
more like a maze than a temple. The building is
made of countless rooms and corridors that can
be rearranged at the press of a button. Only the
psykers of House Ty could ever hope to navigate it.

**Battle Effects:** In a battle where this Spire District
is the stake, fighters are considered to be in partial
cover when out in the open, and considered to be
in full cover when they are in partial cover.

#### Spire District Boon

**Income:** The gang earns 2D6x10 credits from this
Spire District when collecting income.

---

### Grand Cathedral Imperialis

**Jack of Diamonds**

The Grand Cathedral is the jewel in House
Ko'Iron's crown with most electing to meditate
within its walls rather than dwell in their own
palaces. Carved in the shape of a giant Aquila,
almost every surface is gilded and carved with
Imperial holy scripture. It is said that even the
most vile blasphemers can feel the touch of the
divine in the cathedral's air.

**Battle Effects:** In a battle where this Spire District
is the stake, Cawdor gangs generate an additional
D3 Faith Dice during the End phase of each round.
All other gangs can re-roll one hit or wound dice
once per round.

#### Spire District Boon

**Income:** The gang earns D6x10 credits from this
Spire District when collecting income.

#### Enhanced Boon

This Spire District grants Cawdor gangs the
following Boon:

**Income:** A Cawdor gang earns 3D6x10 credits
from this Spire District when collecting income.

### The Vaults of Primus

**Queen of Diamonds**

Few have ever stepped foot within the Vaults
of Primus beyond their Ran Lo auditors.
Though much of the Imperial House's wealth
remains sealed off, those vaults left open
during the nobles' flight from Hive Primus boast
esoteric relics, many of which would immediately
attract the Imperium's ire - were it to ever learn
of their existence.

**Battle Effects:** In a battle where this Spire District
is the stake, any credits awarded by the scenario
are doubled.

#### Spire District Boon

**Income:** The gang earns 3D6x10 credits from this
Spire District when collecting income.

### The Imperial Stair

**King of Diamonds**

The Helmawrs would have their subjects believe
that the Imperial Stair is the only way into
the spire. This is nonsense to any Dome
Runner worth their corpse-starch, though it
holds true for most of the population - and,
more importantly, their supply routes.

**Battle Effects:** In a battle where this Spire District
is the stake, the winner of the Scenario may
recruit a Ganger from their House List for free
during the Update Roster step of the post-battle
sequence.

#### Spire District Boon

**Income:** The gang earns 2D6x10 credits from this
Spire District when collecting income.

---

### Xenos Archives

**Ace of Spades**

By the decree of Terra, every confiscated xenos
weapon should go to the Imperium. Luckily,
what the Imperium doesn't know can't hurt them,
and the Helmawrs skimming a few acquisitions off
the confiscated goods has not yet been noticed.
Each is stashed in the sealed Xenos Archives until
the Helmawrs might have need of them.

**Battle Effects:** In a battle where this Spire District
is the stake, weapons lose the Scarce trait. If they
do not have the Scarce trait, they instead gain the
Plentiful trait.

#### Spire District Boon

**Income:** The gang earns D6x10 credits from this
Spire District when collecting income.

**Special:** The gang treats the Availability Level of
the first Illegal (X) weapon they purchase during
each post-battle sequence as Common.

### Xenos Menageries

**Two of Spades**

A kilometres-wide gilded cage houses the nature
reserves of the noble houses. Strange creatures
from both deep underhive and off-world locales
prowl the synthetic fields. As the noble houses
have gone to ground whilst the War of Succession
rages, it has been a worryingly long time since
these beasts were fed.

**Battle Effects:** In a battle where this Spire District
is the stake, after both crews have deployed,
set up D3+1 Beast's Lair markers starting with
the winner of a roll-off (see page 194 of the
*Necromunda Core Rulebook*).

#### Spire District Boon

**Income:** The gang earns 2D6x10 credits from this
Spire District when collecting income.

**Recruit:** Instead of collecting income, a gang can
choose to recruit an Exotic Beast from their gang
list for free.

### Cinderak Cleansing Hub

**Three of Spades**

As both a popular and ruthlessly bloodthirsty
leader, Lady Cinderak Helmawr had many locales
posthumously take her name. Most famous for
Necromundan nobles is the Cinderak Cleansing
Hub, a great crystal pool of heated water attended
by advanced massage servitors, where Lady
Cinderak was said to spend several hours a day.

**Battle Effects:** In a battle where this Spire District
is the stake, fighters may re-roll failed Willpower
tests.

#### Spire District Boon

**Income:** The gang earns 3D6x10 credits from this
Spire District when collecting income.

### The Shrine of Ancestors

**Four of Spades**

Shrines are often small sites of tribute for their
owners to pay quiet respects. Not so for the
Helmawrs' Shrine of Ancestors, which boasts
ten-foot statues of each past Lord Helmawr carved
from Terran marble. Its ever-burning candles
consume enough promethium daily to power a
small army's worth of flamers.

**Battle Effects:** In a battle where this Spire District
is the stake, weapons with the Blaze trait increase
their Strength and Damage characteristics by 1.

#### Spire District Boon

**Income:** The gang earns 2D6x10 credits from this
Spire District when collecting income.

### Imperial Observatorium

**Five of Spades**

The Imperial Astrologers have not been replaced
since they were sent mad by the Great Darkness,
and were granted Helmawr's peace by the
Enforcers. All that remains is the empty
Observatorium, full of star maps, esoterica,
and bolt-riddled Astrologer corpses.

**Battle Effects:** In a battle where this Spire District
is the stake, each player rolls three D6 and puts
them aside. The results of those dice can each be
used once during the battle to replace any of that
player's to hit, to wound, or save rolls.

#### Spire District Boon

**Income:** The gang earns 2D6x10 credits from this
Spire District when collecting income.

**Recruit:** Instead of collecting income, a gang can
choose to recruit a Sanctioned Indentured Psyker
(see page 102) to their gang for free.

### Helmawr Grand Armoury

**Six of Spades**

Attached to the Imperial Spire like a bulbous
growth is Helmawr's own armoury, that has
been expanded over the centuries to suit his
enormous collection. This bunker boasts walls of
master-crafted guns, ornate pistols and armour,
all custom-made for Gerontius Helmawr's
numerous and very spoilt children.

**Battle Effects:** In a battle where this Spire District
is the stake, each gang can upgrade D3 weapons
to have the Master-crafted trait for free for the
duration of the battle.

#### Spire District Boon

**Special:** The gang may halve the cost of a single
weapon purchased from the Trading Post during
each post-battle sequence, rounded up to the
nearest five credits.

### The Fountain of Night

**Seven of Spades**

The Fountain of Night is the jewel of the Spire
Gardens. It flows with indigo waters and tiny
shards of diamond, like a liquid midnight sky.
There is enough water here to save entire districts
from dehydration - so long as they don't mind the
extra carbon crunch.

**Battle Effects:** In a battle where this Spire District
is the stake, fighters may re-roll failed Cool tests.

#### Spire District Boon

**Income:** The gang earns 3D6x10 credits from this
Spire District when collecting income.

### Chymist Cult Toxin Gardens

**Eight of Spades**

Centuries of alliance between Houses Escher
and Ullanti has produced a rather unique garden,
high up within their palace grounds. Toxic flora
that only grow in natural light are produced here
for the Chymist Cults, provided the Ullanti receive
their cut of elixirs. The beds of these gardens
flourish in purple, blue, and a number of other
eye-burning colours.

**Battle Effects:** In a battle where this Spire District
is the stake, weapons with the Toxin trait increase
their Damage characteristic by 1.

#### Spire District Boon

**Income:** The gang earns D6x10 credits from this
Spire District when collecting income.

#### Enhanced Boon

This Spire District grants Escher gangs the
following Boon:

**Income:** An Escher gang earns 3D6x10 credits
from this Spire District when collecting income.

### The Phyrr Lounge

**Nine of Spades**

Near the base of the spire lies the Phyrr Lounge,
favoured stimm-den of Escher nobility and the
occasional Ullanti brat party. Every chaise longue
is upholstered in Phyrr-leopard pelts and the air
is perfumed enough to feel heavy. Not all the
stimms stored here are strictly legal, but being on
the Imperial Spire's payroll has its perks.

**Battle Effects:** In a battle where this Spire District
is the stake, after both gangs have deployed their
starting crews but before the first roll for Priority,
both players select up to three of their fighters.
The selected fighters gain the Berserker skill for
the duration of the battle if they do not already
have it.

#### Spire District Boon

**Equipment:** While it controls this Spire District,
two fighters in the gang gain stimm-slug stashes
for free.

### The Gris Circenses

**Ten of Spades**

A series of pits line the Gris Dome, one of the
many holdings of House Greim. Here they gather
once a grand cycle and invite the most storied of
Goliath gangers to engage in gory fights to the
death. Those who survive are loaded with enough
creds to last the year and given free entry to next
year's semi-finals.

**Battle Effects:** In a battle where this Spire District
is the stake, fighters may re-roll failed rolls to hit
when using weapons with the Melee trait.
In addition, Goliath gangs subtract 1 from all
Bottle checks.

#### Spire District Boon

**Income:** The gang earns D6x10 credits from this
Spire District when collecting income.

#### Enhanced Boon

This Spire District grants Goliath gangs the
following Boon:

**Income:** A Goliath gang earns 3D6x10 credits
from this Spire District when collecting income.

### The Mercury Palace

Lady Nola Orlock's marriage to Marzus Ran Lo
was one of the most lavish affairs ever seen. The
besotted young man constructed a brand new
palace spire for his bride-to-be. Too bad they were
both poisoned by Delaque agents during the
reception, leaving an architectural wonder full of
silence and unopened wedding gifts.

**Battle Effects:** In a battle where this Spire District
is the stake, an additional four Loot Caskets are
placed upon the battlefield. The gang that opens
the most Loot Caskets during the battle earns an
additional 2D6x10 credits for the battle.

#### Spire District Boon

#### Enhanced Boon

**Income:** An Orlock gang earns 3D6x10 credits
from this Spire District when collecting income.

For many centuries, none besides the Helmawrs
knew of the Delaque chamber nestled in a ruined
dome behind the Imperial Spire. Then the Great
Darkness came, which turned off auspex bafflers
and killed their inter-hive information network.
The Delaque despise their hand being tipped,
and have already relocated elsewhere, leaving
behind a vast but silent mechanical network.

**Battle Effects:** In a battle where this Spire District
is the stake, three fighters belonging to the
challenged player gain the Infiltrate skill for the
duration of the battle if they do not already have it.

#### Spire District Boon

**Income:** The gang earns D6x10 credits from this

A tower of gilded metal is the perfect place
for Primus' biggest producer of tech. The Van Saar
delegates that run it reserve their more interesting
creations for their patrons in the spire, where
nobles will spend thousands of credits just for a
gun that shoots bullets with pink chemtrails or
from a barrel shaped like Lord Helmawr's face.

**Battle Effects:** In a battle where this Spire District
is the stake, fighters can re-roll all failed ammo
tests.

#### Spire District Boon

**Income:** The gang earns D6x10 credits from this
Spire District when collecting income.

**Special:** The first weapon the gang purchases in
each post-battle sequence is considered to be

Availability level.

#### Enhanced Boon

following Boon:

**Income:** A Van Saar gang earns 2D6x10 credits
