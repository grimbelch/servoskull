# The Succession Campaign

[RAW ✔️](/docs/publication-index/rules-index) *The Aranthian Succession – Cinderak Burning, p55*

In the Succession Campaign you pick a side in the war for control over Necromunda.
As the Great Darkness
begins, Necromunda's core cools; the great heat sinks
powering and warming the hives grow cold.

The whole planet is plunged into darkness and in order
to survive, the hive cities and spires cut themselves off
completely from the underhive, leaving those below to
fend for themselves.
