# Narrative Scenarios

[RAW ✔️](/docs/publication-index/rules-index) *The Aranthian Succession – Cinderak Burning, p77*

In this section you will find a selection of 12 narrative
scenarios themed around the events of the Great
Darkness and the start of Lady Credo's rebellion
against House Helmawr. Each of these can either be
played as part of a campaign or as a skirmish battle.
There are also guidance notes included if you wish
to play them as 'historical' scenarios to fight out the
opening salvos of the Necromunda civil war.
