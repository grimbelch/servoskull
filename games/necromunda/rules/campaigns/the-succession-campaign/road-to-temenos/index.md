# Part 2 - Road to Temenos

[RAW ✔️](/docs/publication-index/rules-index) *The Aranthian Succession – The Vaults of Temenos, p60*

Following on from the events of Necromunda: The
Aranthian Succession − Cinderak Burning, the war for
control of Necromunda escalates and spreads out.
As the Great Pilgrimage marches on Hive Temenos,
opportunity arises for gangs to try to seize more
power and influence over the hive world.

With the populace turning to their saints to save them
from the uncertainty of civil war, gangs rush to take
control of their relics − after all, to control the relics is
to control the people.
