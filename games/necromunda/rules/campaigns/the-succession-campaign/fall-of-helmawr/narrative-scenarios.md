# Narrative Scenarios

[RAW ✔️](/docs/publication-index/rules-index) *The Aranthian Succession – The Ruins of Jardlan, p73*

In this section, you will find a selection of 7 narrative
scenarios themed around the events of Lady Haera's
flight from Hive Primus to the depths of Hive
Secundus. Each of these can either be played as part
of a campaign, or as a skirmish battle.
There are also guidance notes included if you wish
to play them as 'historical' scenarios to fight out the
mass attacks of the Ash Waste Nomads as Haera flees
across Necromunda.
