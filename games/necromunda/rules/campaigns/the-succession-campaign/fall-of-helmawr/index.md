# Part 3 - Fall of Helmawr

[RAW ✔️](/docs/publication-index/rules-index) *The Aranthian Succession – The Ruins of Jardlan, p50*

Following on from the events of Necromunda: The
Aranthian Succession − The Vaults of Temenos, House
Aranthus has returned to Necromunda and Lady
Haera, the current Lord Helmawr, must flee for her life
as the spire is invaded by her enemies and her noble
allies turn against her.

As the forces of Ozostium and his prophet secure
their power in the Palatine Cluster, Lady Haera
desperately tries to make her way to the Dust Wall
and the loyal Enforcers stationed there. With all eyes
looking for her, gangs rush to take control of the area
between Primus and Secundus hoping to either aid
Lady Haera's flight or capture her.
