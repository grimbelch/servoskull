# Wasteland Workshop

[RAW ✔️](/docs/publication-index/rules-index) *Book of the Outlands, p58*

Out in the ash wastes, a gang's vehicles are its life.
Getting stranded outside the shadow of a hive city,
between settlements, or worse, somewhere in the
deep wastes, is often a quick way to a slow death. It is
understandable then that gangs operating outside the
hives cherish their vehicles, to the point of considering
each to be another trusted member of the gang.

Drivers will name their rides and rigs, adorn them with
trophies and ornaments or paint them in outlandish
colours, and go back to save them even when some
newbie juve might be bleeding out at their feet. This
behaviour is much like the extreme styles adopted by
Clan House gangs in the hive cities – a way to stand
out, be noticed and make sure their enemies know just
who is smashing their face into the dust.
