**Skills:** Impetuous, Nerves of Steel, Hurl
**Wargear:** None
**Special Rules:** Dramatis Personae, Mother of Sumpkrocs, Scaly Hide, UnpredictableSpecial Rules

**Dramatis Personae:** A House Goliath gang may hire Old Three-eyes in the same way as any other Dramatis Personae. However, it is important to note that Old Three-eyes is neither a Bounty Hunter or a Hive Scum, and therefore is not subject to any of the special rules associated with Bounty Hunters or Hive Scum.

**Mother of Sumpkrocs:** Any Sumpkrocs within 6" of Old Three-Eyes can use her Cool and Willpower characteristics instead of their own.

**Scaly Hide:** Old Three-eyes has a thick scaly hide, granting her a 4+ save roll.

**Unpredictable:** Old Three-eyes cannot be included in Group Activations, nor can she benefit from the Leading by Example rule. In addition, all her attacks have the Reckless trait.
