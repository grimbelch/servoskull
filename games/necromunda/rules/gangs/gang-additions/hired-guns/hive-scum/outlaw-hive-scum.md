#### The Gorvos Twins

When founding an Underhive Outcast gang they can be led by Gaen 'The Gunk Queen' Gorvos and have her
brother Vunder Gorvos as a Champion. To do so, apply the following effects:

- Gaen 'The Gunk Queen' Gorvos is the gang's Leader. She has the Criminal Organisation Affiliation and Mastermind Archetype.
- Vunder Gorvos is one of the gang's Champions. He has the Survivor Archetype.
- All members of the gang may take [gunk bombs](/docs/trading-post/grenades#gunk-bomb) for 20 credits each.

## Nomad Outcast

***Ash Waste Nomads only***

Nomad Outcasts and Hive Scum

In many places the rules may tell you to add a Hive Scum to your gang. If running an Ash Waste Nomads gang, whenever instructed to add a Hive Scum instead add a Nomad Outcast.

A gang may recruit up to five Nomad Outcasts at a time.

| M | WS | BS | S | T | W | I | A | Ld | Cl | Wil | Int |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 5" | 4+ | 4+ | 3 | 3 | 1 | 4+ | 1 | 8+ | 8+ | 8+ | 8+ |

**Special Rules:** Outlaw, "You Get What You Pay For"Special Rules

**Outlaw:** A Nomad Outcast is an Outlaw.

**“You Get What You Pay For”:** Unlike other fighters, Nomad Outcasts are not counted during the Choose Crew step of the pre-battle sequence. Instead, at the end of this step, any Nomad Outcasts available to the gang may be added to the crew, regardless of the Crew Selection method in use. This may take the number of fighters in a starting crew above the number specified by the scenario.

Equipment

Nomad Outcasts are equipped with an ash cloak and sky mantle, and may purchase up to 60 credits worth of weapons and Wargear from the Nomad Outcast equipment list. They may be armed with up to three weapons, only one of which may have the Unwieldy trait, and this counts as two weapons choices. Note that the value of these weapons and Wargear should be included when determining Gang Rating.
