### FAQ

**Q. What happens when a Venator gang rolls either
one or two 6s for their Settlement Territory?**  
A. If a Venator gang rolls a 6 for their Settlement
Territory, then they do not gain any new fighters.
However, if they manage to roll two 6s they may hire
a single Ganger of their choice from those available
to the gang. This does mean they could gain a free
Ogryn Hunter!

**Q. Can a Venator Hunt Leader still be upgraded
to have either the Sanctioned Psyker or Nonsanctioned
Psyker rule as described in Necromunda:
Book of the Outcast?**  
A. Yes.
