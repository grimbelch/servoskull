The content of the Errata and Frequently Asked Questions relative to this gang in *The Book of Ruin* is presented here for completeness and transparency only, as these changes have already been incorporated into their respective entries.

### ERRATA

**PAGE 43 – CULT ALPHA (LEADER)**  
Amend the final sentence of the Cult Alpha’s EXTRA
ARM special rule as follows:

Additionally, a Cult Alpha with an Extra Arm may carry
a fourth weapon and may attack with three weapons
with the Sidearm and/or Melee traits rather than the
usual two.

**PAGE 47 – PSYCHIC FAMILIAR – OMEN OF FORTUNE**  
Change the start of the second paragraph from ‘Whilst
the Familiar is within…’ to ‘Whilst a Familiar is within…’.

### FAQ

**Q. If a fighter has multiple Exotic Beasts with the
Omen of Fortune rule, can they use this ability to
avoid multiple hits?**  
A. No, they may only attempt to avoid one hit per round
regardless of the number of Exotic Beasts with the
Omen of Fortune ability they have.
