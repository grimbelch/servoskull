### FAQ

**Q. How and when can I select the new wyrd powers
from Book of the Outcast for my fighters that have
been upgraded with Psychoteric Whispers?**  
A. When the Psychoteric Whispers upgrade is
purchased for a fighter, the specialisation and first
power must be selected from the Madness, Delusion
or Darkness disciplines. However, during campaign
play, when purchasing additional powers with XP they
can be selected from any discipline. Note that the
Psychoteric Choirs rule only applies to powers from the
Madness, Delusion or Darkness disciplines.
