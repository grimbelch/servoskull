# Spyrer Equipment

[RAW ✔️](/docs/publication-index/rules-index) *The Book of Desolation, p130*

### Bolt Launcher

*The classic armament of the Orrus pattern of Spyrer
suit, bolt launchers unleash a devastating salvo
of firepower.*

**Tier 1 Augmentation:** Increase the Damage characteristic to 2.  
**Tier 2 Augmentation:** Increase the AP characteristic to -2.  
**Tier 3 Augmentation:** Replace the Rapid Fire (1) trait with Rapid Fire (2).

### Disintegration Matrix

*Only faintly understood and dangerous in the extreme
to use, the disintegration matrix affords unparalleled
destructive power to the Spyrer brave (or foolish)
enough to equip their hunting rig with them.*

**Tier 1 Augmentation:** Remove the Scarce trait.  
**Tier 2 Augmentation:** Increase the AP characteristic to -3.  
**Tier 3 Augmentation:** Increase the Damage characteristic to 3.

### Jakara Hunting Rig

*The lightest of the Spyre hunting rigs, favouring speed
and agility over heavy armour, the Jakara hunting rig
is fashioned from materials designed to minimise any
restrictions upon the wearer's movements.*

A Jakara hunting rig grants its wearer a 5+
armour save.

**Tier 1 Augmentation:** Increase the wearer's Strength characteristic by 1.  
**Tier 2 Augmentation:** Increase the wearer's Attack characteristic by 1.

### Malcadon Hunting Rig

*With added support around the upper torso, the
Malcadon hunting rig is adapted to absorb the extra
strain placed upon the wearer by constantly moving
themselves using the inbuilt grapnel launcher.*

A Malcadon hunting rig grants its wearer a 5+
armour save.

**Tier 1 Augmentation:** Increase either the wearer's Ballistic Skill or Weapon Skill characteristic by 1.  
**Tier 2 Augmentation:** Increase the armour save to 4+.

### Malcadon Slashing Claw And Paired Malcadon Slashing Claws

*The primary armament of the Malcadon Spyre Hunter,
Malcadon slashing claws are often the last thing their
prey sees as they sweep down to finish off enemies
trapped within the webs they launch.*

**Tier 1 Augmentation:** Increase the AP characteristic to -3.  
**Tier 2 Augmentation:** Increase the Damage characteristic to 2.  
**Tier 3 Augmentation:** Increase the Strength characteristic to S+1.

### Malcadon Toxin Whip And Paired Malcadon Toxin Whips

*Occasionally used as an alternative loadout by
Malcadon Spyre Hunters, Malcadon Toxic Whips
deliver deadly chemicals, incapacitating the target for
later elimination.*

**Tier 1 Augmentation:** Improve the Long Range accuracy modifier to -.  
**Tier 2 Augmentation:** Add the Shield Breaker trait.  
**Tier 3 Augmentation:** Treat the Toughness of any
fighters hit by this weapon as being one less for the
purpose of the Toxin trait. This effect stacks with
a chem-synth.

### Mirror Shield

*The Jakara mirror shield is a complex device that
contains an unusual force field generator. The
generator sets up a resonant energy vortex across the
shield which bleeds off the power it absorbs into one
of several containment devices which stud the shield's
surface. In turn, this stored energy can be channelled
back to its source as a brilliant bolt of heat and light.*

**Tier 1 Augmentation:** Gain a 6+ field armour save against any attack from within the fighter's vision arc.  
**Tier 2 Augmentation:** Increase the Long Range characteristic to 12".  
**Tier 3 Augmentation:** Gain a 5+ field armour save against any attack from within the fighter's vision arc.

### Monomolecular Sword

*The monomolecular sword carried by the Jakara is a
marvel of engineering. The blade is a living crystalline
structure which constantly renews its edge as it is
blunted. The sword's edge is only one molecule thick
and can slice through all but the densest of matter.
As the Jakara's power boosts kick in, the sword's edge
renews itself more regularly and evenly to make it ever
sharper still.*

**Tier 1 Augmentation:** Increase the Damage characteristic to 2.  
**Tier 2 Augmentation:** Increase the AP characteristic to -3.  
**Tier 3 Augmentation:** Increase the Strength characteristic to S+2.

### Naga Blade

*An alternative armament to the monomolecular sword
for the Jakara, the naga blade is used when more
reach is needed and to pin opponents in place.*

**Tier 1 Augmentation:** Add the Shock trait.  
**Tier 2 Augmentation:** Increase the AP characteristic to -2.  
**Tier 3 Augmentation:** Increase the Damage characteristic to 3.

### Orrus Hunting Rig

*Constructed of many overlapping layers of armoured
plating with a built-in force field projector, an
Orrus hunting rig grants the wearer a large degree
of protection.*

An Orrus hunting rig grants its wearer a 4+ armour
save and a 6+ field armour save.

**Tier 1 Augmentation:** Increase the field armour save to 5+.  
**Tier 2 Augmentation:** Increase the field armour save to 4+.

### Orrus Power Fists

*The power fists of the standard Orrus Spyre Hunter
loadout are capable of pulverising any opposition into
a pulp.*

**Tier 1 Augmentation:** Add the Pulverise trait.  
**Tier 2 Augmentation:** Increase the AP characteristic to -2.  
**Tier 3 Augmentation:** Increase the Damage characteristic to 3.

### Orrus Power Talons

*An alternative loadout for the Orrus hunting rig, power
talons are adept at punching through armour and
crushing the body beneath, making them ideally suited
to killing Malstrain Genestealers.*

**Tier 1 Augmentation:** Add the Rending trait.  
**Tier 2 Augmentation:** Increase the Damage characteristic to 2.  
**Tier 3 Augmentation:** Increase the Strength characteristic to S+2.

### Sovereign Hunting Rig

*Only worn by the most favoured scions of the Helmawr
line, the Sovereign hunting rig is able to mount the
armaments of the Jakara, Malcadon and Yeld hunting
rigs, allowing combinations unavailable to other
Spyre Hunters.*

A Sovereign hunting rig grants its wearer a 5+ armour
save and a 6+ field armour save.

**Tier 1 Augmentation:** Increase the field armour save to 5+.  
**Tier 2 Augmentation:** Increase the wearer's Strength characteristic by 1.

### Volkite Discharger

*Utilising ancient Martian technology, volkite
dischargers are rare on Necromunda and only the
wealthiest scions of House Helmawr can afford to have
them fitted to their Spyre hunting rigs.*

**Tier 1 Augmentation:** Increase the Damage characteristic to 2.  
**Tier 2 Augmentation:** Increase the AP characteristic to -1.  
**Tier 3 Augmentation:** Increase the Long Range characteristic to 12".

### Web Incisors And Paired Web Incisors

*Able to shoot out silky thread of gossamer, which then
harden to the strength of steel wires, the Malcadon's
Embiopteran pattern web incisors leave their opponents
bound and helpless.*

**Tier 1 Augmentation:** Increase the Long Range characteristic to 10".  
**Tier 2 Augmentation:** Increase the Strength characteristic to 4.  
**Tier 3 Augmentation:** Increase the Long Range characteristic to 12".

### Yeld Hunting Rig

*Formed from especially light, yet hardy materials, the
Yeld hunting rig is crafted from amazingly light yet
strong metallic compounds, allowing its wearer to
glide through the air when combined with the wings
built into the rig. If the wearer does not move then the
armour takes on chameleonic properties, hiding the
Yeld's position.*

A Yeld hunting rig grants its wearer a 5+ armour save.
In addition, if the wearer does not move during their
activation, then until the start of their next activation
any ranged attacks targeting them suffer a -1 to
hit modifier.

**Tier 1 Augmentation:** Increase the wearer's Movement characteristic by 1".  
**Tier 2 Augmentation:** Ranged attacks targeting the wearer suffer a -1
to hit modifier even if the wearer moved during
their activation.

### Yeld Laser Gauntlet And Paired Yeld Laser Gauntlets

*The Yeld hunting rig is fitted with heavy gauntlets
mounting multiple laser tubes. The gauntlets fire
a storm of laser bolts, a deadly attack that only
becomes more so as the suit routes more power to the
gauntlets, allowing them to maintain a longer burst
of fire.*

**Tier 1 Augmentation:** Increase the AP characteristic to -2.  
**Tier 2 Augmentation:** Increase the Long Range characteristic to 24".  
**Tier 3 Augmentation:** Increase the Strength characteristic to 4.

### Yeld Missile Gauntlet And Paired Yeld Missile Gauntlets

*An alternative loadout to the Yeld laser gauntlets,
the Yeld missile gauntlets fire a cluster of high yield
munitions at the enemy and grant the Yeld one of the
longest ranges of all Spyrer weapons.*

**Tier 1 Augmentation:** Increase the AP characteristic to -1.  
**Tier 2 Augmentation:** Increase the Strength characteristic to 6.  
**Tier 3 Augmentation:** Add the Blast (3") trait.

### Yeld Wings

*Made from hundreds of individual plates of metal
connected together by micro-fibre bundles on an
articulated frame, Yeld wings allow the wearer to glide
gracefully down and with effort even gain in height.
During combat, a series of conductive filaments from
the wearer's cortex allows the Yeld to attack with their
razor-sharp edges and deflect enemy strikes away.*

A Spyrer equipped with Yeld wings ignores all terrain,
may move freely between levels without restriction,
and can never fall. They may not, however, ignore
impassable terrain and may not end their movement
with their base overlapping an obstacle or another
fighter's base.

**Tier 1 Augmentation:** Add the Parry trait.  
**Tier 2 Augmentation:** Increase the Damage to 2.  
**Tier 3 Augmentation:** Add the Knockback trait.

### Medusian Projectors

[RAW ✔️](/docs/publication-index/rules-index) *The Aranthian Succession – Spire of Primus, p122*

Composed of ancient technology, Medusian projectors
emit petrifying beams of light that paralyse every cell
in a target’s body. The light is concentrated through a
series of lenses and shot as a beam of chilling energy.

**Tier 1 Augmentation:** Increase the Ammo
characteristic to 2+.

**Tier 2 Augmentation:** Increase the Long Range
characteristic to 16".

**Tier 3 Augmentation:** Gain the Cursed trait.

### Sthenian Claw

[RAW ✔️](/docs/publication-index/rules-index) *The Aranthian Succession – Spire of Primus, p122*

The huge claws of a Sthenian rig are sharp and
hard enough to cut through toughened plasteel.
Just the lightest touch with these blades can sever
vital arteries.

**Tier 1 Augmentation:** Increase the Strength
characteristic to 4.

**Tier 2 Augmentation:** Increase the Damage
characteristic to 2.

**Tier 3 Augmentation:** Increase the AP
characteristic to -2.

### Sthenian Hunting Rig

[RAW ✔️](/docs/publication-index/rules-index) *The Aranthian Succession – Spire of Primus, p122*

So rare are the Sthenian hunting rigs that many
consider them to be myth. These are the original
rigs of the ancient Helmw’ars, incorporating tech
lost to the Dark Age of Technology and sealed away
from the prying eyes of the Cult Mechanicus within
centuries-old vaults.

A Sthenian hunting rig grants its wearer a 5+ armour
save and a 6+ field armour save.

**Tier 1 Augmentation:** Increase the armour save to 4+.

**Tier 2 Augmentation:** Increase the field armour save
to 5+.
