# Malstrain Wyrd Powers

[RAW ✔️](/docs/publication-index/rules-index) *The Book of Desolation, p55*

Malstrain Coalescences and any Malstrain Corrupted Gang Leader upgraded to have
access to Wyrd Powers have access to the Malstrain Wyrd Powers listed below, and
treat these powers as one of their Primary skill sets.

## 1. Catalyst (Basic), Continuous Effect

Whenever a friendly fighter within 12" of this
model is taken Out of Action, they may perform a free Shoot (Basic) or Fight (Basic)
action before being removed from the battlefield.

## 2. Hypnotic Gaze (Double)

Target an enemy fighter within 9", if they are Standing
and Ready, they lose their Ready marker.

## 3. The Horror (Basic)

Target an enemy fighter within 12", the targeted fighter must
immediately make a Nerve test.

## 4. Leech Essence (Basic)

Target an enemy fighter within 12", they suffer a S3,
AP-1, D1 hit. If they take any damage as a result of this attack then the wyrd may
either regain a Wound lost earlier in the battle or remove one Flesh Wound.

## 5. Paroxysm (Basic), Continuous Effect

Target an enemy fighter within 9", while
this power is maintained they set their BS and WS to 6+.

## 6. Aura of Despair (Basic), Continuous Effect

While this power is maintained, all
enemy models within 12" reduce their Cool characteristic by 1.
