**Members of the Ordo Ultima**
The Cultists of the _Ordo Ultima_ are far from their home territory of
Nuln. So, they nurse drinks, trying their best not to look suspicious
as they watch for Rechtshandler, occasionally muttering made-up
prayers to Verena to cement their cover as travelling scholars. Each
cultist has a letter O enclosed within a letter U tattooed on the left

breast, and all talk with urbane Nulner accents.
