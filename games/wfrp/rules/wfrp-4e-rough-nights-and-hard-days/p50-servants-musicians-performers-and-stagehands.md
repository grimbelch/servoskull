**The Dammenblatz Agents**

Baron Otto von Dammenblatz has ordered his two spies
in Nuln - Brocks and Reiner - to finally put an end to his
Liebwitz problem. Both have gritty, street accents, and neither are
especially good at their jobs. If Dominique is still alive, she is sent
to lead them, which she immediately resents and hates, given she
prefers to work alone. She cuts her hair short for the assignment
