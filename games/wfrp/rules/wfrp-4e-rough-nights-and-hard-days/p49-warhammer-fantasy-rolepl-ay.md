**Brecht Kavenner, Lawyer**
Nasal-voiced Kavenner is the Countess's personal lawyer. Known
as a stern, dedicated man who is utterly devoted to his wife,
children, and the Cult of Verena, he uses this good reputation to
disguise his second life as one of the most successful and depraved
cultists of the _Ordo Ultima_ . Tasked three years ago to corrupt the
Countess to the service of the Prince of Pleasure, he has found
her inexplicably intractable. This confuses and annoys him in
equal measure, which he invariably takes out on his inferiors.

**Royal Servants and Guards**
The Countess goes nowhere without her many servants, attendants,
and guards. Contrary to popular rumour, they are not all bronzed
gods and goddesses, chosen because they amuse the Countess's
taste. However, most _are_ drawn from the the lesser Noble Houses
of Nuln as commoners do not normally attend royalty.
