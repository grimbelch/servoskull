**Bruno Franke**

Bruno, the gravin's judicial champion, is a tower of muscle
topped with a smiling face that hints at mischief. His Altdorf
accent is deep - he was born in the imperial capital to parents
of Southlander extraction almost forty years ago. He has a
distinctive, infectious laugh, and he loves a good challenge.

**Gustaf Rechtshandler**

A droning self-important man with a polished accent, Gustaf
is in his late 50s. He adores attention, so takes any opportunity
to interrupt conversations to show off his knowledge. Knowing
nothing about the topic at hand is no impediment - he simply
paraphrases what others say around him as if they were his ideas.
