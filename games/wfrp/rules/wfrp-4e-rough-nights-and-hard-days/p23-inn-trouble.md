# Inn Trouble

Seedling is a Halfling from the distinguished Thorncobble clan
of Altdorf. She's currently on the run from her responsibilities,
and is looking to earn a little coin with gambling and gossip.
Like most Thorncobbles, she has impeccable diction, and an
upper-class accent that surprises those used to meeting more
earthy Halflings.
